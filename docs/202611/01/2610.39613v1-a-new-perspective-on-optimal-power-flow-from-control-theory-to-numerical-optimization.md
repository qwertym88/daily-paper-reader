## 摘要
最优潮流（OPF）问题是一个具有非线性约束和多个潜在解的经典非凸优化问题。现有方法往往无法找到全局最优解，这促使人们开发能够识别多个局部解的方法。本文应用最优控制原理求解OPF问题，旨在单次运行中系统性地探索所有可行解。我们首先利用罚函数法和拉格朗日乘子处理OPF问题中的约束，然后将其重新表述为最优控制问题。通过极大值原理建立两个问题之间的联系。此外，提出了一种数值算法来求解该最优控制问题。系统的状态轨迹能够快速且稳定地收敛到OPF问题的解。通过设计初始控制序列，所提方法能够成功找到多个解并摆脱局部解。最后，在若干代表性测试系统上进行了仿真，以验证所提方法在识别非凸问题多解方面的潜力。

## Abstract
The optimal power flow (OPF) problem is a classical non-convex optimization problem with nonlinear constraints and multiple potential solutions. Existing methods often fail to find the global optimal solution, motivating the development of a method to identify multiple local solutions. This paper applies the optimal control principle to solve the OPF problem, aiming to systematically explore all feasible solutions within a single run. We first handle the constraints in the OPF problem using the penalty method and the Lagrange multiplier, and then reformulate it as an optimal control problem. The connection between the two problems is established by the maximum principle. Moreover, a numerical algorithm is proposed to solve this optimal control problem. The state trajectory of the system can rapidly and stably converge to the solution of the OPF problem. By designing the initial control sequence, the proposed method can successfully find multiple solutions and escape local solutions. Finally, simulations were conducted on several representative test systems to validate the potential of the proposed method in identifying multiple solutions for non-convex problems.
