## 摘要
本文研究实Hilbert空间中光滑非线性凸-凹鞍点问题的Tikhonov正则化二阶原始-对偶动力系统。该系统包含消失阻尼系数 α/t、Hessian驱动项以及临界Tikhonov正则化系数 c/t^2。通过构造合适的Lyapunov函数，我们在相同参数条件下建立了到最小范数鞍点的强收敛性和快速收敛率，且无需施加任何最终的球内/球外条件。具体而言，原始-对偶轨线强收敛到最小范数鞍点，而原始-对偶间隙和速度范数分别以 o(t^{-2}) 和 o(t^{-1}) 的速率衰减。此外，我们提出一种带有可计算相对残差准则的不精确原始-对偶算法，并证明所生成序列强收敛到同一最小范数鞍点，同时原始-对偶间隙具有 o(k^{-2}) 的收敛率。最后，在不同维数的非线性和双线性问题上的数值实验说明了最小范数选择性质，并比较了所提动力系统与算法和相关方法的性能。

## Abstract
In this paper, we study a Tikhonov-regularized second-order primal--dual dynamical system for smooth nonlinear convex--concave saddle-point problems in real Hilbert spaces. The dynamical system incorporates a vanishing damping coefficient $α/t$, a Hessian-driven term, and a critical Tikhonov regularization coefficient $c/t^2$. By constructing suitable Lyapunov functions, we establish strong convergence to the minimum-norm saddle point and fast convergence rates under the same parameter conditions, without imposing any eventual inside/outside-ball condition. Specifically, the primal--dual trajectory converges strongly to the minimum-norm saddle point, while the primal--dual gap and the velocity norm decay at rates $o(t^{-2})$ and $o(t^{-1})$, respectively. Furthermore, we propose an inexact primal--dual algorithm with a computable relative residual criterion and prove strong convergence of the generated sequence to the same minimum-norm saddle point, together with an $o(k^{-2})$ convergence rate for the primal--dual gap. Finally, numerical experiments on nonlinear and bilinear problems of different dimensions illustrate the minimum-norm selection property and compare the performance of the proposed dynamical system and algorithm with related methods.
