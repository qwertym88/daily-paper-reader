## 摘要
我们研究带不定权重的体制切换扩散的条件平均场型线性二次最优控制问题。其动力学和成本依赖于在给定马尔可夫链历史的条件下状态与控制的条件期望。在标准定号性条件下，我们建立最优控制的存在唯一性，并通过两组耦合Riccati方程和一个由马尔可夫链驱动的线性倒向随机微分方程给出其反馈表示和最优成本。为处理不定权重情形，我们将等价成本泛函方法推广到该设定，利用依赖于体制的二次调整项作用于状态的条件均值及其与该均值的偏差。当等价成本泛函中的变换权重满足标准定号性条件时，该构造给出原Hamilton系统唯一可解性以及唯一最优控制的反馈表示。所得结果还为建立不满足某些现有单调性条件的全耦合线性条件平均场正倒向随机微分方程的可解性提供了一种有效方法。我们进一步将结果应用于不完全体制切换市场中带外生负债的条件均值-方差投资组合选择，并得到最优投资策略。

## Abstract
We study a conditional mean-field type linear quadratic optimal control problem for regime-switching diffusions with indefinite weights. The dynamics and cost depend on conditional expectations of the state and control given the history of the Markov chain. Under standard definiteness conditions, we establish the existence and uniqueness of the optimal control and derive its feedback representation and optimal cost through two sets of coupled Riccati equations and a linear backward stochastic differential equation driven by the Markov chain. To treat the case of indefinite weights, we extend the equivalent cost functional approach to this setting using regime-dependent quadratic adjustments for the conditional mean of the state and its deviation from that mean. When the transformed weights in the equivalent cost functional satisfy standard definiteness conditions, this construction yields unique solvability of the original Hamiltonian system and a feedback representation of the unique optimal control. The proposed results also provide an effective method for establishing the solvability of fully coupled linear conditional mean-field forward-backward stochastic differential equations that do not satisfy certain existing monotonicity conditions. We further apply the results to conditional mean-variance portfolio selection with exogenous liability in an incomplete regime-switching market and derive the optimal investment strategy.
