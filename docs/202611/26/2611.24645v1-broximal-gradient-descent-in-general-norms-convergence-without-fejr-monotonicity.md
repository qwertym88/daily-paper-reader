## 摘要
我们研究一般范数下的 Broximal 梯度下降，用于闭凸集上的光滑优化。每次更新在可行集与一个范数球的交集上最小化线性模型。与半径适当受限的欧氏情形不同，即使范数平方是强凸的，到解的距离也可能增加。我们转而使用比较器不等式和可观测的平稳性证书。对于凸目标，给定半径可得到值收敛，包括当下确界不可达时的逃逸，以及在没有距离界假设下的最后迭代 O(K^{-1/3}) 界。水平集距离界在校准半径下给出全迭代 O(1/K) 速率，并在半径不使用问题常数时给出渐近 O(\log K/K) 速率。强凸性和幂增长给出显式的更快包络和迭代收敛。在没有凸性时，约束 Polyak--Łojasiewicz 不等式在给定半径下给出线性收敛。一种可观测的半径减半规则需要一个有效的光滑常数，但既不需要 PL 模数，也不需要初始间隙界；热启动在逆精度上给出对数总 oracle 复杂度。该分析将已建立的局部 oracle 要素与水平集控制、半径校准以及基于精度的 oracle 计数区分开来。

## Abstract
We study Broximal Gradient Descent in a general norm for smooth optimization over a closed convex set. Each update minimizes the linear model over the intersection of the feasible set and a norm ball. Unlike the Euclidean case with suitably restricted radii, the distance to a solution can increase, even when the squared norm is strongly convex. We instead use a comparator inequality and an observable stationarity certificate. For convex objectives, prescribed radii yield value convergence, including escape when the infimum is unattained, and a last-iterate $O(K^{-1/3})$ bound without a distance-bound assumption. A level-set distance bound gives an all-iteration $O(1/K)$ rate with calibrated radii and an asymptotic $O(\log K/K)$ rate with radii using no problem constant. Strong convexity and power growth give explicit faster envelopes and convergence of the iterates. Without convexity, a constrained Polyak--Łojasiewicz inequality gives linear convergence with prescribed radii. An observable radius-halving rule needs a valid smoothness constant but neither the PL modulus nor an initial gap bound; warm starting gives logarithmic total oracle complexity in inverse accuracy. The analysis separates established local-oracle ingredients from the control of level sets, radius calibration, and accuracy-based oracle counts.
