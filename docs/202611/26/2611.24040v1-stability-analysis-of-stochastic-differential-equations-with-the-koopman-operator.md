## 摘要
本文通过Koopman算子框架研究随机系统的稳定性。特别地，我们建立了Koopman半群的收敛性质与底层随机系统的特定稳定性性质之间的联系。此外，我们证明了特定的Koopman特征函数能够刻画随机吸引性，并用两个数值例子说明这些结果。这项工作与现有的基于Koopman算子的确定性系统稳定性结果相呼应，并将其推广到随机框架。

## Abstract
This paper investigates the stability of stochastic systems through the lens of the Koopman operator framework. In particular, we establish the connection between the convergence properties of the Koopman semigroup and specific stability properties of the underlying stochastic system. Moreover, we demonstrate that specific Koopman eigenfunctions capture stochastic attractivity properties and we illustrate these results with two numerical examples. This work mirrors existing Koopman operator-based stability results for deterministic systems and extends them to the stochastic framework.
