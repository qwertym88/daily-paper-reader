## 摘要
高度参数化的卷积神经网络通常具有强大的预测性能，但其内存、计算和部署成本促使人们寻求从大型参考模型中恢复紧凑架构的方法。我们提出SoU-RHS，一种贝叶斯结构化稀疏方法，它在正则化马蹄形收缩（Regularized Horseshoe Shrinkage, RHS）的基础上引入了所谓的不确定性来源稀疏全局-局部机制（Sources of Uncertainty Sparse Global-Local mechanism, SoU-SGL）。所得模型增强了活跃滤波器与非活跃滤波器之间的区分度，为架构恢复提供了一种有效机制。SoU-RHS的一个关键特性是稳定性：在不同的过参数化初始宽度下，以及在合理的剪枝阈值范围内，它恢复出的紧凑架构具有相近的总规模和逐层分配。这支持如下解释：该过程识别的是适应数据的紧凑结构，而不仅仅是分别压缩每个初始模型。在标准图像分类基准上，SoU-RHS恢复出的架构显著小于确定性结构化稀疏基线，同时保持有竞争力的准确率，在每种评估配置下均获得更低的负对数似然，并且通常具有更低的期望校准误差。

## Abstract
Highly parameterized convolutional neural networks often achieve strong predictive performance, but their memory, computation, and deployment costs motivate the search for methods that recover compact architectures from large reference models. We propose SoU-RHS, a Bayesian structured-sparsity procedure that augments Regularized Horseshoe Shrinkage (RHS) with the so-called Sources of Uncertainty Sparse Global-Local mechanism (SoU-SGL). The resulting model sharpens the separation between active and inactive filters, providing an effective mechanism for architecture recovery. A key property of SoU-RHS is stability: across different overparameterized starting widths and under a reasonable range of pruning thresholds, it recovers compact architectures with similar total size and layer-wise allocation. This supports the interpretation that the procedure is identifying a compact structure adapted to the data, rather than merely compressing each initial model separately. Across standard image-classification benchmarks, SoU-RHS recovers substantially smaller architectures than deterministic structured-sparsity baselines, while maintaining competitive accuracy, achieving lower negative log-likelihood in every evaluated configuration and generally lower expected calibration error.
