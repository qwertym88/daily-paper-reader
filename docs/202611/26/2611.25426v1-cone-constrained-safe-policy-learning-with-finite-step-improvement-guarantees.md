## 摘要
安全强化学习中的一个核心挑战是在提升任务性能的同时降低不可行风险，尤其是当长期约束后果难以表示和优化时。我们提出约束聚合函数（CAF），这是一种面向可行性的可学习长时程风险表示，它满足折扣贝尔曼递归并具有唯一不动点。所提出的 CAF 导出一种风险优势，用于衡量相对于当前策略的预测不安全性的降低量。基于该表示，我们开发了锥约束策略优化（CCPO），利用 CAF 诱导的风险优势定义风险下降方向。所提出的 CCPO 通过梯度投影解决任务上升与风险下降之间的冲突，从构造的安全锥的相对内部选择最终更新。在标准平滑性假设和步长界下，该更新保证任务改进与风险降低同时发生。在数值上，CCPO 在紧急制动中保持可行，平均最优性差距为 1.05%，并在安全关键基准上实现较低的约束违反。

## Abstract
One central challenge in safe reinforcement learning is improving task performance while reducing infeasibility risk, especially when long-term constraint consequences are difficult to represent and optimize. We propose the Constraint Aggregation Function (CAF), a learnable feasibility-oriented representation of long-horizon risk that satisfies a discounted Bellman recursion and admits a unique fixed point. The proposed CAF induces a risk advantage that measures the reduction in predicted unsafety relative to the current policy. Building on this representation, we develop Cone-Constrained Policy Optimization (CCPO), which uses the CAF-induced risk advantage to define a risk descent direction. The proposed CCPO resolves conflicts between task ascent and risk descent through gradient projection, selecting the final update from the relative interior of a constructed safety cone. Under standard smoothness assumptions and a step-size bound, this update guarantees simultaneous task improvement and risk reduction. Numerically, CCPO stays feasible with a 1.05% mean optimality gap in emergency braking and attains low constraint violations on safety-critical benchmarks.
