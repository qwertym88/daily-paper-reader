## 摘要
多智能体系统上的线性二次调节（LQR）问题通常要求分布式反馈增益受网络拓扑结构约束，从而导致不可避免的次优性差距。现有研究要么给出使凸松弛成立的系统与拓扑条件，要么通过策略梯度算法寻求驻点。本文通过采用动作依赖策略（ADP）来缩小次优性差距，其中每个智能体的控制输入不仅依赖于邻居智能体的状态，还依赖于其他一些智能体的控制输入。我们表明，尽管ADP呈现分布式结构，但它能够扩大反馈增益的可行集，并在适当条件下甚至可表示集中式最优策略。然而，这以代价函数失去强制性以及投影梯度流（PGF）可能无法收敛为代价。为克服这一困难，我们提出一种部分正则化形式，其保证PGF收敛，并建立标准多智能体LQR与其动作依赖对应物之间的联系。我们还建立了在PGF下局部收敛到全局最优策略的充分条件，且与正则化无关。最后，给出数值例子验证ADP的性能。

## Abstract
The linear quadratic regulation (LQR) problem over a multi-agent system usually requires a distributed feedback gain that is structurally constrained by the network topology, incurring an inevitable suboptimality gap. Existing studies either identify conditions on the system and topology that enable convex relaxation or seek a stationary point via policy gradient algorithms. In this paper, we reduce the suboptimality gap by adopting action-dependent policies (ADPs), where the control input of each agent depends on not only states of neighbor agents, but also control inputs of some other agents. We show that although exhibiting a distributed structure, ADP can enlarge the feasible set of feedback gains and even represent centralized optimal policies under suitable conditions. However, this comes at the price of losing coercivity of the cost function and possible failure of convergence for the projected gradient flow (PGF). To overcome this difficulty, we propose a partially regularized formulation, which guarantees convergence of PGFs, and forges a connection between standard multi-agent LQR and its action-dependent counterpart. Sufficient conditions for local convergence to globally optimal policies under PGFs regardless of regularization are also established. Finally, numerical examples are presented to validate the performance of ADP.
