## 摘要
本文提出一种新的时序逻辑，我们称之为Koopman时序逻辑（KTL），旨在形成一种形式化方法，其中Koopman算子理论为验证和控制具有非线性动态的系统提供数学途径。我们首先回顾信号时序逻辑，然后介绍KTL，包括其语法、布尔语义和定量语义。接下来，我们提出一种由KTL描述的控制规范出发的模型预测控制（MPC）构建方法。通过将所提出的MPC构建方法应用于AC-DC功率变换的可靠设计，评估了其有效性和计算效率。

## Abstract
This paper proposes a novel temporal logic, which we call Koopman Temporal Logic (KTL), towards a formal method in which Koopman operator theory provides a mathematical way to verify and control systems with nonlinear dynamics. We first review signal temporal logic and then introduce KTL, including its syntax, Boolean semantics, and quantitative semantics. Next, we propose a formulation of Model Predictive Control (MPC) from a control specification described by KTL. Effectiveness and computational efficiency of the proposed MPC formulation are evaluated by applying it to a reliable design of AC-DC power conversion.
