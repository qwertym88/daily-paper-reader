## 摘要
学习可靠的控制策略需要考虑转移动力学中的不确定性，以及函数估计和动作生成引入的误差。我们针对具有连续状态和动作、且名义转移律未知的折扣鲁棒马尔可夫决策过程，提出一种 Actor-Critic 方法。我们考虑由 Wasserstein 距离、全变差、Kullback--Leibler 散度或 Pearson 卡方散度定义的转移不确定性集合。将一个条件扩散模型一次性拟合到离线转移数据上。在随后的每次迭代中，我们使用共享目标的极小极大准则从当前策略观测中估计鲁棒动作价值函数，然后通过指数加权得分匹配更新生成式 actor。正则化保持探索，并提供条件分布估计所需的密度正则性。在覆盖性和正则性条件下，我们为评论家估计、学习到的条件动作律以及所实施策略的鲁棒回报建立了有限样本界。这些界区分了评论家估计误差、名义转移模型中的不确定性以及策略正则化，并刻画了四个鲁棒性半径如何影响所得的保证。

## Abstract
Learning reliable control policies requires accounting for uncertainty in transition dynamics and errors introduced by function estimation and action generation. We develop an actor--critic method for discounted robust Markov decision processes with continuous states and actions and an unknown nominal transition law. We consider transition uncertainty sets defined by Wasserstein distance, total variation, Kullback--Leibler divergence, or Pearson chi-square divergence. A conditional diffusion model is fitted once to offline transitions. At each subsequent iteration, we estimate the robust action-value function from current-policy observations using a shared-target minimax criterion, then update a generative actor through exponentially weighted score matching. Regularization preserves exploration and provides the density regularity needed for conditional distribution estimation. Under coverage and regularity conditions, we establish finite-sample bounds for critic estimation, the learned conditional action law, and the robust return of the implemented policy. The bounds distinguish critic estimation error, uncertainty in the nominal transition model, and policy regularization, and characterize how the four robustness radii affect the resulting guarantees.
