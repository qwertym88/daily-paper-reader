## 摘要
联邦强化学习（FRL）为在可访问多个可并行运行的边缘设备时加速强化学习训练提供了一种有前景的途径。最近研究表明，即使这些边缘设备访问不同的动态模型——例如具有不同系统配置的汽车或机器人——也能实现与设备数量成正比的线性加速的最优收敛速率。然而，该结果要求算法步长的选择方式依赖于未知模型参数。此外，它仅适用于折扣设置，而有观点认为该设置比持续控制任务更适合分幕式任务。本文针对带平均奖励的异构联邦强化学习获得了有限时间界。我们表明，即使采用与底层动态无关的通用步长选择，也能实现最优收敛速率和线性加速。为实现该结果，我们将现有单时间尺度FRL方法修改为一种新颖的双时间尺度变体，并额外引入迭代平均。我们指出，该思想还可推广到文献中已研究的其他设置，例如同构FRL或具有折扣代价函数的异构情形。

## Abstract
Federated Reinforcement Learning (FRL) provides a promising way to speedup training in reinforcement learning when one has access to multiple edge devices that can operate in parallel. Recently, it has been shown that even when these edge devices have access to different dynamic models---such as cars or robots with differing system configurations---an optimal convergence rate that has a linear speedup proportional to the number of devices is achievable. However, this result requires that the algorithm's stepsize be chosen in a manner that depends on the unknown model parameters. Also, it applies only to a discounted setting, which has been argued to fit episodic tasks better than continuing control tasks. In this paper, we obtain finite-time bounds for heterogeneous FRL with average rewards. We show that optimal convergence rate and linear speedup are achievable even with a universal stepsize choice, independent of the underlying dynamics. To achieve our result, we modify the existing one-timescale FRL method to a novel two-timescale variant that additionally incorporates iterate averaging. We remark that this idea also extends to other settings already studied in the literature, such as the homogeneous FRL or the heterogeneous case with a discounted cost function.
