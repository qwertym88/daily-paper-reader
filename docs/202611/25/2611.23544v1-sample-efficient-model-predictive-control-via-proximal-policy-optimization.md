## 摘要
基于采样的模型预测控制（MPC）能够实现对复杂非线性系统的实时控制，但由于需要重复进行轨迹仿真，往往会产生较高的计算成本。本文在强化学习中的策略优化与基于采样的MPC之间建立联系，通过样本复用提升计算效率。从轨迹优化的KL正则化分布形式出发，我们推导出一个基于带符号指数优势函数的精确性能差恒等式。该恒等式启发了两种算法：PPO-MPPI和SPO-MPPI，它们将近端策略优化技术适配到基于采样的MPC中，从而能够利用先前评估过的轨迹进行多次优化更新，而无需额外的前向仿真。我们为这两种方法建立了群体水平的单调改进保证，并证明了在固定协方差高斯采样分布下PPO-MPPI收敛到一阶驻点。在四足机器人速度跟踪和四旋翼飞行器路径跟踪上的实验表明，所提方法在计算成本显著低于多阶段采样方法的情况下，仍能取得具有竞争力的控制性能。我们的结果在策略优化与基于采样的MPC之间建立了具有理论依据的联系，并凸显了轨迹复用在样本高效预测控制中的潜力。

## Abstract
Sampling-based model predictive control (MPC) enables real-time control of complex nonlinear systems but often incurs substantial computational costs due to repeated trajectory simulations. In this paper, we bridge policy optimization in reinforcement learning and sampling-based MPC to improve computational efficiency through sample reuse. Starting from a KL-regularized distributional formulation of trajectory optimization, we derive an exact performance-difference identity based on a signed exponential advantage function. This identity motivates two algorithms, PPO-MPPI and SPO-MPPI, which adapt proximal policy optimization techniques to sampling-based MPC, enabling multiple optimization updates using previously evaluated trajectories without additional forward simulations. We establish population-level monotonic improvement guarantees for both methods and prove convergence to first-order stationarity for PPO-MPPI under fixed-covariance Gaussian sampling distributions. Experiments on quadruped velocity tracking and quadrotor path tracking demonstrate that the proposed methods achieve competitive control performance at substantially lower computational cost than multi-stage sampling approaches. Our results establish a principled connection between policy optimization and sampling-based MPC and highlight the potential of trajectory reuse for sample-efficient predictive control.
