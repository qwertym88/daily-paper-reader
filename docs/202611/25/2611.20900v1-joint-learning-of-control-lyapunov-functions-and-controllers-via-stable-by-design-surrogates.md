## 摘要
训练和验证基于神经网络的控制李雅普诺夫函数（CLFs）可能带来较大的计算开销。为应对这一挑战，我们提出一个框架，利用结构化神经网络和基于投影的参数化方法联合学习 CLF 与镇定控制器。具体而言，我们采用 Polyak-Lojasiewicz 网络（PLNets），其参数化非凸、正定的 CLF 候选函数，并构造闭环动力学的设计即稳定代理模型，该代理模型在构造上满足李雅普诺夫下降条件。该控制器将线性状态反馈与一个类似 Sontag 通用公式的高阶修正项相结合，并通过训练使所得闭环动力学与设计即稳定代理模型对齐。与基于 Sontag 公式的经典方法不同，我们的框架不需要已知的 CLF；相反，它同时学习 CLF 和控制器。我们为所学控制器建立了局部和全局稳定性保证，并通过数值示例证明了所提方法的有效性。

## Abstract
Training and verifying neural network-based control Lyapunov functions (CLFs) can be computationally demanding. To address this challenge, we propose a framework that jointly learns a CLF and a stabilizing controller using structured neural networks and a projection-based parameterization. Specifically, we employ Polyak-Lojasiewicz networks (PLNets), which parameterize nonconvex, positive-definite CLF candidates, and construct a stable-by-design surrogate of the closed-loop dynamics that satisfies the Lyapunov decrease condition by construction. The controller combines linear state feedback with a higher-order correction term resembling Sontag's universal formula and is trained to align the resulting closed-loop dynamics with the stable-by-design surrogate. Unlike classical approaches based on Sontag's formula, our framework does not require a known CLF; instead, it learns the CLF and controller simultaneously. We establish local and global stability guarantees for the learned controller and demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed approach through numerical examples.
