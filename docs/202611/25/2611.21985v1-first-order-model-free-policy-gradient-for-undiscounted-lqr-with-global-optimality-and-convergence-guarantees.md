## 摘要
我们提出了一个一阶无模型策略梯度（PG）框架，用于学习无折扣无限时域线性二次调节器（LQR）策略，并具有全局最优性和收敛性保证。我们首先构造了一个由LQR增益参数化的随机包装策略类，以支持围绕确定性线性控制律进行探索。我们分析了在无限时域上由探索引起的额外代价，并建立了一个弱梯度支配条件，用以刻画探索下的全局最优性。然后，我们利用采样轨迹信息构造了一种无模型一阶PG算法，无需辨识系统动力学或近似值函数。最后，我们建立了有限时间收敛保证，给出了所提PG算法达到近全局最优性所需的充分样本数。

## Abstract
We provide a first-order model-free policy gradient (PG) framework for learning undiscounted infinite-horizon linear quadratic regulator (LQR) policies with global optimality and convergence guarantees. We first construct a stochastic wrapper policy class parameterized by the LQR gain to enable exploration around a deterministic linear control law. We analyze the additional cost induced by exploration over infinite-horizon, and establish a weak gradient dominance condition that characterizes global optimality under exploration. We then construct a model-free first-order PG algorithm using sampled trajectory information, without identifying the dynamics or approximating a value function. Finally, we establish finite-time convergence guarantees, providing the sufficient number of samples required for near-global optimality with our proposed PG algorithms.
