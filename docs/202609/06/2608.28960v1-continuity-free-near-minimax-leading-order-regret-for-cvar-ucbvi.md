## 摘要
对于有限时域表格型CVaR强化学习，先前的工作证明了对于任意归一化收益分布，遗憾界为$\widetilde{O}(τ^{-1}\sqrt{SAK})$，并在密度下界条件下得到更优的$\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{SAK/τ})$速率。我们证明相同的Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI算法在无连续性假设下也能达到更优速率。关键在于一个选择性预算自界：回合短缺的条件方差至多为τ加上值估计宽度。将其代入原始Bernstein分解中，可高概率地得到任意归一化收益分布（包括原子分布、混合分布和连续分布）下的遗憾界$\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{SAK/τ}+(SAHK^{1/4}+S^2AH)/τ)$。主导项$τ^{-1/2}$与期望遗憾的最小最大下界在对数因子内匹配。因此，Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI在主导阶机制下在整个收益分布类上是最小最大最优的；低阶项仍保留其$τ^{-1}$依赖性。

## Abstract
For finite-horizon tabular CVaR reinforcement learning, prior work proves a $\widetilde{O}(τ^{-1}\sqrt{SAK})$ leading regret bound for arbitrary normalized return laws and the sharper $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{SAK/τ})$ rate under a density lower bound. We show that the same Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI algorithm attains the sharper rate without continuity assumptions. The key is a selected-budget self-bound: the conditional variance of the episode shortfall is at most $τ$ plus the value-estimation width. Substitution into the original Bernstein decomposition yields, with high probability, $\widetilde{O}(\sqrt{SAK/τ}+(SAHK^{1/4}+S^2AH)/τ)$ regret for arbitrary normalized return laws, including atomic, mixed, and continuous laws. The $τ^{-1/2}$ leading term matches the expected-regret minimax lower bound up to logarithmic factors. Thus Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI is minimax-optimal over the full return-law class in the leading-order regime; the lower-order terms retain their $τ^{-1}$ dependence.
