CONTINUITY-FREE NEAR-MINIMAX LEADING-ORDER REGRET FOR CVAR-UCBVI 

YUANLONG CHEN 

Abstract. For finite-horizon tabular CVaR reinforcement learning, Wang et al. [6] prove a ˜O(τ −1√SAK ) leading regret bound for arbitrary normalized return laws and the sharper ˜O(√SAK/τ ) rate under a density lower bound. We show that the same Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI algorithm attains the sharper rate without continuity assumptions. The key is a selected-budget self-bound: the conditional variance of the episode shortfall is at most τ plus the value-estimation width. Sub-stitution into the original Bernstein decomposition yields, with high probability, 

˜O(√SAK/τ +( SAHK 1/4+S2AH )/τ ) regret for arbitrary normalized return laws— atomic, mixed, or continuous. The τ −1/2 leading term matches the expected-regret minimax lower bound of Wang et al. [6] up to logarithmic factors. Thus Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI is minimax-optimal over the full return-law class in the leading-order regime; the lower-order terms retain their τ −1 dependence.

1.1. Related work. Bastani et al. [2] give regret guarantees for a broad class of static, trajectory-level risk criteria. Wang et al. [6] specialize to precommitted CVaR and obtain the bounds compared in table 1. Wang et al. [7] reduce optimized cer-tainty equivalents to risk-neutral RL; their sharp CVaR specialization still assumes continuously distributed returns with a density lower bound [7, Assumption D.11 and Theorem D.12]. Ni et al. [4] study reward-free exploration for CVaR, which is a different data-collection protocol and a PAC rather than online-regret objective. Liang and Luo [3] analyze dynamic, recursively composed risk measures, which are time-consistent but differ from the static CVaR of the full trajectory studied here. This paper addresses the complementary question of removing the continuity con-dition from the regret analysis of Bernstein CVaR-UCBVI for static trajectory-level CVaR. 2.
