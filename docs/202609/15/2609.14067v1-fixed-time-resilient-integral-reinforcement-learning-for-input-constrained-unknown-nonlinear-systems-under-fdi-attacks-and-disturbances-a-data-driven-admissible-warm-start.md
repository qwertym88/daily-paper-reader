## 摘要
本文针对在执行器限制、虚假数据注入攻击和外部扰动下运行的未知非线性系统，提出了一种弹性学习控制器。其核心思想是直接从有限轨迹数据中学习饱和安全策略，同时保证学习误差和闭环状态均在独立于初始条件的一致固定时间内收敛到紧集邻域。积分形式从可实现的学习律中消除了未知漂移，而存储的信息数据在在线激励衰减后仍能维持学习。为减轻闭环对任意评论家初始化的敏感性，通过有限维Koopman表示提升部署前数据（这些数据也可从回放栈中复用），以构造镇定初始策略；其逆饱和策略映射提供了数据驱动的评论家权重热启动。由此得到的控制器在构造上保持输入约束，并在持续攻击和扰动下保证实用的固定时间鲁棒性。所提出的学习与初始化架构进一步通过双连杆机器人镇定示例进行验证，结果表明：状态恢复迅速、评论家学习有界、执行器约束可靠满足，并且在信息引导的评论家初始化下闭环行为得到改善。

## Abstract
This paper develops a resilient learning controller for unknown nonlinear systems operating under actuator limits, false-data-injection attacks, and external disturbances. The key idea is to learn a saturated secure policy directly from finite trajectory data while guaranteeing that both the learning error and the closed-loop state converge to compact neighborhoods within a uniform fixed time independent of initial conditions. An integral formulation removes the unknown drift from the implementable learning law, while stored informative data sustain learning after online excitation fades. To mitigate the closed-loop sensitivity to arbitrary critic initialization, pre-deployment data, which may also be reused from the replay stack, are lifted through a finite-dimensional Koopman representation to construct a stabilizing initial policy, whose inverse saturated-policy map provides a data-driven critic-weight warm start. The resulting controller preserves input constraints by construction and guarantees practical fixed-time robustness under persistent attacks and disturbances. The proposed learning and initialization architecture is further verified through a two-link robot stabilization example, where the results demonstrate rapid state recovery, bounded critic learning, reliable actuator-constraint satisfaction, and improved closed-loop behavior under informed critic initialization.

### 2.2 关键技术细节
- **系统模型**：\(\dot x=f(x)+g(x)(u+a)+d\)，其中 \(u\) 为受约束控制，\(a\) 为匹配FDI攻击，\(d\) 为外部扰动；控制满足逐分量 \(|u_j|<\lambda\)。
- **安全HJI与非二次惩罚**：输入惩罚包含 \(\tanh^{-1}\) 结构，最优控制为  
  \(u^*(x)=-\lambda\tanh(\frac{1}{2\lambda}R^{-1}g^\top(x)\nabla V^*(x))\)，因此 \(|u_j^*|<\lambda\)。攻击与扰动最优策略分别由 \(\nabla V^*\) 经 \(T^{-1},S^{-1}\) 生成。
- **积分Bellman–Isaacs恒等式**：  
  \(V^*(x(t))-V^*(x(t-\Delta T))+\int_{t-\Delta T}^t \ell d\tau=0\)。该式只依赖状态增量、策略和可测运行成本积分，不显式包含未知 \(f(x)\)。
- **评论家近似**：\(V^*(x)=W^\top\phi(x)+\varepsilon(x)\)，估计 \(\hat V=\hat W^\top\phi\)。实现策略由 \(\hat W\) 经饱和映射生成，构造上保持输入约束。
- **有限经验回放**：存储有限样本 \((\Delta\phi_k,\hat R_k)\)，构造回放Gramian \(\Psi=\sum\psi_k\psi_k^\top\)，要求 \(\lambda_{\min}(\Psi)\ge\lambda>0\)，即有限数据激励条件。
- **两幂评论家更新**：  
  \(\dot{\hat W}=-\Gamma\psi(t)(|s|^q\mathrm{sgn}(s)+|s|^r\mathrm{sgn}(s))-\Gamma\sum_k\psi_k(|s_k|^q\mathrm{sgn}(s_k)+|s_k|^r\mathrm{sgn}(s_k))-\sigma\Gamma\hat W\)，其中 \(0<q<1<r\)。泄漏项可选，主要增强鲁棒性。
- **固定时间稳定性**：评论家误差满足 \(\dot V_W\le -h_qV_W^\mu-h_rV_W^\nu+\Delta_W\)，进入时间与 \(\hat W(0)\) 无关。闭环分析采用移动窗口值函数能量 \(V_I=\int_{t-\Delta T}^t V^*(x)d\tau\) 与评论家能量 \(V_W\) 的混合Lyapunov函数，证明 \(x\) 与 \(\hat W\) 实用固定时间一致最终有界。
- **Koopman离线热启动**：离线轨迹经提升 \(\eta=K(x)\) 识别有限维模型 \((A_K,B_K)\)，设计镇定策略 \(u_K=-K\eta\)，再通过逆饱和策略关系  
  \(G(x)\hat W\approx -2\lambda R\,\mathrm{artanh}(\lambda^{-1}u_K)\)  
  拟合得到 \(\hat W_K\)，并构造容许初始化区域 \(W_0^{adm}\)。可用Chebyshev中心选取鲁棒单点初始化。Koopman模型仅用于离线热启动，不替代在线HJI学习。
