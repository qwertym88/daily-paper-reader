## 摘要
从数字重建放射影像（DRRs）中进行骨选择性合成，需要在保留解剖细节的同时将骨骼信号与覆盖组织分离。U-Net跳跃连接可提供精细的编码器特征，但其传递独立于解码器上下文。我们提出RFS-UNet，使解码器参与高分辨率通道重校准。汇聚后的编码器与解码器特征共同预测一个有界残差尺度，其初始化旨在保留原始跳跃传递。该模块在两个最精细的分辨率上运行，并直接集成到骨干网络中。在重建的患者唯一队列上，RFS较U-Net-64将测试PSNR提升0.10 dB。与仅编码器重校准的三随机种子比较，支持了解码器上下文的贡献。RFS还提供了CBAM的低延迟替代方案，在匹配分析中推理速度快2.25倍。因此，解码器条件化的复用通过一种紧凑的架构改动改进了骨选择性合成。

## Abstract
Bone-selective synthesis from digitally reconstructed radiographs (DRRs) requires separating skeletal signal from overlying tissue while preserving anatomical detail. U-Net skip connections supply fine encoder features, but their transfer is independent of decoder context. We introduce RFS-UNet, which lets the decoder participate in high-resolution channel recalibration. Pooled encoder and decoder features jointly predict a bounded residual scale, initialized to preserve the original skip transfer. The module operates at the two finest resolutions and integrates directly into the backbone. On a rebuilt patient-unique cohort, RFS improves test PSNR over U-Net-64 by 0.10 dB. A three-seed comparison with encoder-only recalibration supports the contribution of decoder context. RFS also offers a lower-latency alternative to CBAM, with 2.25 times faster inference in matched profiling. Decoder-conditioned reuse thus improves bone-selective synthesis through a compact architectural change.
