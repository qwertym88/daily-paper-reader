## 摘要
软演员-评论家（SAC）被广泛用于连续动作空间中的熵正则化强化学习，实际实现仅朝着不断演变的目标执行少量演员步骤。本文中，我们证明了当目标策略来自策略镜像下降时的收敛保证，并将其与经典的 Gibbs 目标进行比较。我们推导出演员目标强凸性和光滑性的充分条件，其特征由通过 Legendre 微分算子刻画的 $Q$ 函数估计的曲率所决定，并建立了在演员和评论家近似误差下达到 $\mathcal{O}\!\left(N^{-\frac{1}{5}}\right)$ 的最佳迭代有限时间收敛速率。此外，镜像下降步长 $\lambda$ 直接控制目标漂移，从而控制演员跟踪误差，而相应的 Gibbs 界则包含一个不消失的跟踪项。

## Abstract
Soft Actor-Critic (SAC) is widely used for entropy-regularised reinforcement learning with continuous action spaces, and practical implementations perform only a few actor steps towards an evolving target. In this work, we prove convergence guarantees when the target policy arises from policy mirror descent and compare it with the classical Gibbs target. We derive sufficient conditions for the strong convexity and smoothness of the actor objective, characterised by the curvature of the $Q$-function estimate through the Legendre differential operator, and establish an $\mathcal{O}\!\left(N^{-\frac{1}{5}}\right)$ best-iterate finite-time convergence rate up to actor and critic approximation errors. Moreover, the mirror-descent step size $λ$ directly controls the target drift and hence actor tracking error, whereas the analogous Gibbs bound contains a non-vanishing tracking term.

## 2. 方法论

### 2.1 问题设定与策略类

- 考虑无限时域熵正则 MDP：\((S,A,P,c,\gamma)\)，动作空间 \(A=(-1,1)^M\)，正则化参数 \(\tau>0\)。
- 正则化价值函数：
  \[
  V^\pi_\tau(s)=\mathbb E^\pi_s\left[\sum_{n=0}^\infty \gamma^n\big(c(s_n,a_n)-\tau H(\pi(\cdot|s_n))\big)\right].
  \]
- 策略类为 tanh 压缩高斯：
  - 特征映射 \(x:S\to\mathbb R^{d\times M}\)，\(\|x(s)\|_{\rm op}\le 1\)；
  - 均值 \(m_w(s)=x(s)^\top w\)，参数 \(w\in B(R)\)；
  - 高斯核 \(q_w(\cdot|s)=\mathcal N(m_w(s),\Sigma)\)；
  - 策略 \(\pi_w=(\tanh)_\# q_w\)。
- 该形式化对应实际 SAC 中常用的“固定协方差、线性均值、tanh 压缩”策略。

### 2.2 目标策略：Gibbs 目标与镜像下降目标

- 演员目标为对重放状态分布 \(\rho_n\) 的反向 KL：
  \[
  J_n(w,\pi_{\rm target})=\int_S \mathrm{KL}(\pi_w(\cdot|s)\|\pi_{\rm target}(\cdot|s))\rho_n(ds).
  \]
- **Gibbs 目标**：
  \[
  \frac{d\pi^G_{n+1}}{d\Lambda}(s,a)=\frac1{Z_n(s)}\exp\left(-\frac1\tau \hat Q_n(s,a)\right).
  \]
- **策略镜像下降目标**：
  \[
  \pi_{n+1}(\cdot|s)=\arg\min_{m\in\mathcal P(A)}
  \left\{
  \int_A\left(\hat Q_n(s,a)+\tau\log\frac{d\pi_n}{d\Lambda}(s,a)\right)m(da)
  +\frac1\lambda \mathrm{KL}(m\|\pi_n(\cdot|s))
  \right\}.
  \]
  闭式解为：
  \[
  \frac{d\pi_{n+1}}{d\Lambda}(s,a)
  \propto
  \left(\frac{d\pi_n}{d\Lambda}(s,a)\right)^{1-\tau\lambda}
  \exp\left(-\lambda \hat Q_n(s,a)\right).
  \]
- 当 \(\lambda=1/\tau\) 时，镜像下降目标退化为经典 soft policy improvement / Gibbs 目标。
