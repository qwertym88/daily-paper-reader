## 摘要
我们提出了一种在结构化环境中随机系统最优控制的理论，该环境通过成本泛函中的惩罚项来表征。我们表明，此类控制问题通常具有尖锐的有限时间转变，这种转变与临界时间处控制策略的定性变化相关联。从过阻尼朗之万方程和二次成本泛函出发，我们证明所有由此产生的问题都属于三个典型等价类（抛物型、双曲型和椭圆型），这些类由控制哈密顿量行列式的符号区分。对于每一类，我们获得了最优协议、成本函数和临界时间的闭式解，并表明该转变在平均场水平上表现出连续相变的特征。然后，我们建立了最优控制成本与势阱淬火后控制非平衡弛豫的大偏差速率函数之间的映射关系。该映射涵盖抛物型和双曲型，而椭圆型没有简单的弛豫对应物。这种对应关系意味着，最近发现的有限时间动力学相变（直接采样成本指数级高）可以通过对最优控制轨迹的普通平均来访问。为验证我们的理论发现，我们报告了三个利用光镊捕获胶体粒子的实验：平均随机功的控制转变，以及自由扩散和谐波弛豫中的有限时间动力学相变。

## Abstract
We present a theory for the optimal control of stochastic systems in structured environments, represented by penalty terms in the cost functional. We show that such control problems generically feature sharp finite-time transitions associated with a qualitative change in the control strategy at a critical time. Starting from an overdamped Langevin equation and a quadratic cost functional, we show that all resulting problems fall into three canonical equivalence classes (parabolic, hyperbolic, and elliptic), distinguished by the sign of the determinant of the control Hamiltonian. For each class, we obtain the optimal protocol, the cost function, and the critical time in closed form, and show that the transition exhibits features of a continuous phase transition at mean-field level. We then establish a mapping between the optimal control cost and the large-deviation rate function governing non-equilibrium relaxation after a potential quench. The mapping covers the parabolic and hyperbolic classes, while the elliptic class has no simple relaxation counterpart. This correspondence implies that recently discovered finite-time dynamical phase transitions, which are exponentially costly to sample directly, are accessible through ordinary averages over optimally controlled trajectories. To validate our theoretical findings, we report three experiments with optically trapped colloidal particles: a control transition for the mean stochastic work, and the finite-time dynamical phase transitions in free diffusion and in harmonic relaxation.
