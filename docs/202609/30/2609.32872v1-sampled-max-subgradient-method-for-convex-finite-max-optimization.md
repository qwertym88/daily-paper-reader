## 摘要
我们研究用于大规模凸有限极大问题的采样最大值次梯度方法（SMax-SGM）。每次迭代在一个从 N 个分量中新抽取的随机子集上取最大值，并对采样最大值点取一个次梯度。因此，该方法是在采样最大值替代函数上的随机次梯度下降。我们用平均前 k 间隙加上错过所有前 k 个分量的概率来界定替代误差。局部化论证仅需在近优下水平集上考虑这些量。在有界次梯度矩假设下，这给出 O(ε^{-2}) 次梯度查询；并且，当该集合中有 k 个分量仍近乎活跃时，给出 Õ((N/k)ε^{-2}) 次分量值查询，且以全扫描代价为上限。反之，一个一维仿射构造表明，即使平均前 k 间隙处处消失且次梯度查询不受限制，Ω(N/k) 次分量值查询也可能是必要的。张量网格特例解释了子集大小何时可以独立于网格基数。在具有 2×10^5 和 5×10^6 个分量的有限极大值问题上的实验表明，在所述协议下，SMax-SGM 达到 5% 数值参考差距时，相比每个达到目标的对比方法，使用更少的分量值查询和更低的优化器时间。

## Abstract
We study the Sampled-Max Subgradient Method (SMax-SGM) for large convex finite-max problems. Each iteration maximizes over a fresh random subset of the $N$ components and takes one subgradient of the sampled maximizer. The method is therefore stochastic subgradient descent on a sampled-max surrogate. We bound the surrogate error by an average-top-$k$ gap plus the probability of missing all top-$k$ components. A localization argument requires these quantities only on a near-optimal sublevel set. Under a bounded subgradient-moment assumption, this yields $O(\varepsilon^{-2})$ subgradient queries and, when $k$ components remain nearly active in that set, $\widetilde O((N/k)\varepsilon^{-2})$ component-value queries, capped by the full-scan cost. Conversely, a one-dimensional affine construction shows that $Ω(N/k)$ component-value queries can be necessary even when the average-top-$k$ gap vanishes everywhere and subgradient queries are unlimited. A tensor-grid specialization explains when the subset size can become independent of grid cardinality. Experiments on finite maxima with $2\times10^5$ and $5\times10^6$ components show that, under the reported protocols, SMax-SGM reaches a 5% numerical-reference gap with fewer component-value queries and lower optimizer time than each comparison method that reaches the target.
