## 摘要
我们提出了一种面向异构多加速器系统的调度框架，用于执行硬实时（HRT）与软实时且具有QoS感知的DNN推理工作负载。该框架将在线强化学习（RL）调度器与确定性的运行时安全层相结合。RL调度器通过联合选择子作业优先级和加速器分配来动态生成候选调度方案，而运行时认证可保证无论学习到的策略如何，所有HRT截止时间均能得到满足。

为在异构且具有优先级约束的系统中实现运行时认证，我们引入了一种基于逐机器切分的可调度性保持前缀替换准则。该准则保证运行时生成的调度前缀能够保持可行后续调度的存在性，而无需相对于某个可行参考调度满足完整的完成时间支配关系。

在异构DNN加速器平台上的实验结果表明，与最先进的调度方法相比，所提框架在保持HRT可调度性的同时，提高了QoS感知的截止时间满足率、客户端级服务质量和加速器利用率。

## Abstract
We present a scheduling framework for heterogeneous multi-accelerator systems executing both hard real-time and soft real-time QoS-aware DNN inference workloads. The framework combines an online reinforcement learning (RL) scheduler with a deterministic runtime safety layer. The RL scheduler dynamically generates candidate schedules by jointly selecting sub-job priorities and accelerator assignments, while runtime certification guarantees that all HRT deadlines remain satisfied independently of the learned policy.   To enable runtime certification in heterogeneous precedence-constrained systems, we introduce a schedulability-preserving prefix replacement criterion based on machine-wise cuts. The proposed criterion guarantees that runtime-generated schedule prefixes preserve the existence of a feasible continuation without requiring complete finish-time dominance with respect to a feasible reference schedule.   Experimental results on heterogeneous DNN accelerator platforms show that the proposed framework preserves HRT schedulability while improving QoS-aware deadline satisfaction, client-level service quality, and accelerator utilization compared to state-of-the-art scheduling approaches.
