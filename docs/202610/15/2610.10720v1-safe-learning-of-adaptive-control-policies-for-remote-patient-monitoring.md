## 摘要
远程患者监测（RPM）能够对患者在其日常环境中进行连续观察，从而改善健康结局和生活质量。RPM的一个关键挑战是在平衡患者安全与监测成本的同时，确定最优监测强度。当系统参数（如转移概率和成本）最初未知时，该问题会变得更加复杂。我们开发了一个基于学习的控制框架，用于估计这些参数并实时调整监测策略。所提出的方法是一种面向RPM的在线基于模型的强化学习算法，在探索过程中明确优先考虑患者安全。我们提供了安全性和收敛到最优策略的理论保证。仿真结果表明，该算法收敛到最优的基于阈值的策略，保持较低的治疗成本，并降低患者达到危重健康状态的风险。

## Abstract
Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) enables continuous observation of patients in their daily environments, improving both health outcomes and quality of life. A key challenge in RPM is determining the optimal monitoring intensity, while balancing patient safety and monitoring costs. This problem is further complicated when system parameters, such as transition probabilities and costs, are initially unknown. We develop a learning-based control framework that estimates these parameters and adapts the monitoring policy in real time. The proposed approach is an online model-based reinforcement learning algorithm tailored to RPM, with patient safety explicitly prioritized during exploration. We provide theoretical guarantees on safety and convergence to the optimal policy. Simulation results show that the algorithm converges to the optimal threshold-based policy, maintains low treatment costs, and reduces the risk of patients reaching critical health states.
