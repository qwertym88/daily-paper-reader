## 摘要
物理连接的人形机器人需要一致的动力学，以处理内部执行器回路、机器人间作用力以及变化的地面支撑。我们通过逐构件动力学装配扩展 RoboCompiler，从刚体、运动执行器部件和折算转子惯量中编译出约简动力学。编译得到的体雅可比和解析约束曲率产生显式科里奥利因子，满足浮动基准速度的功率恒等式。两级约简在组合支撑与手部连接的同时保留可复用的机器人模型；局部惯性更新的秩至多为六，且给定加速度的约束力旋量关于执行器作用量呈仿射关系。这些接口支持执行器感知跟踪、耦合力分配、负载更新以及五台手部连接人形机器人的支撑模式切换。在八种人形机器人配置的 264 个可行状态中，归一化加速度偏差相对于硬约束参考保持在 $1.6\!\times\!10^{-10}$ 以内，相对于编译器无关参考保持在 $1.4\!\times\!10^{-10}$ 以内；MuJoCo 仿真在环形舞蹈中达到 0.73~mm 的骨盆均方根（RMS）误差，并完成经历 20 次支撑模式切换的侧步。

## Abstract
Physically connected humanoids need consistent dynamics for internal actuator loops, inter-robot forces, and changing ground supports. We extend RoboCompiler with member-wise dynamics assembly, compiling reduced dynamics from rigid bodies, moving actuator parts, and reflected rotor inertias. Compiled body Jacobians and analytic constraint curvature yield an explicit Coriolis factor satisfying the power identity for floating-base quasi-velocities. A two-level reduction retains reusable robot models while composing their supports and hand connections; local inertial updates have rank at most six, and prescribed-acceleration constraint wrenches are affine in actuator efforts. These interfaces support actuator-aware tracking, coupled force allocation, payload updates, and support-mode switching for five hand-linked humanoids. Across 264 feasible states in eight humanoid configurations, normalised acceleration discrepancies remain within $1.6\!\times\!10^{-10}$ against a hard-constraint reference and $1.4\!\times\!10^{-10}$ against a compiler-independent reference; MuJoCo simulations achieve 0.73~mm root-mean-square (RMS) pelvis error in the ring dance and complete side steps through 20 support-mode changes.
