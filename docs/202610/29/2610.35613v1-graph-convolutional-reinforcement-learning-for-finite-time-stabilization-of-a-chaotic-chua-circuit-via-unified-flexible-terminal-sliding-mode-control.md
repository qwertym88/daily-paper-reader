## 摘要
本文提出一种图卷积强化学习框架，用于物理蔡氏电路中混沌动力学的有限时间镇定。该电路直接以电容电压、电感电流和物理元件参数进行建模，从而保持了数学模型与底层电气系统之间的联系。针对集总模型中的不确定性、外部扰动以及有界控制输入，设计了一种统一柔性终端滑模控制器，以在有限时间内实现镇定。该控制器引入状态相关的非线性指数，在避免平衡点附近奇异项的同时提供灵活的收敛特性。为减少对人工控制器整定的依赖，利用采用图卷积网络的执行器-评论家强化学习方案优化容许控制器参数，该网络利用了电路动力学的耦合结构。学习得到的参数被约束在理论稳定性条件所要求的范围内。数值实验表明，该方法能够快速抑制混沌振荡，并使状态变量和滑模变量在有限时间内收敛，同时满足规定的输入饱和限制。与参考控制方法相比，所提方法将 IAE、ISE、ITAE 和 ITSE 分别降低了 56.27%、56.40%、61.69% 和 64.70%。这些结果表明，基于图的强化学习能够在保留底层滑模控制器鲁棒性和解析结构的同时有效整定参数。

## Abstract
This paper introduces a graph convolutional reinforcement learning framework for the finite-time stabilization of chaotic dynamics in a physical Chua circuit. The circuit is modeled directly in terms of capacitor voltages, inductor current, and physical component parameters. This preserves the connection between the mathematical model and the underlying electrical system. A unified, flexible terminal sliding-mode controller is developed to achieve stabilization within a finite time in the presence of uncertainties in the lumped model, external disturbances, and bounded control inputs. The controller incorporates state-dependent nonlinear exponents to provide flexible convergence characteristics while avoiding singular terms near equilibrium. To minimize dependence on manual controller tuning, the admissible controller parameters are optimized using an actor-critic reinforcement learning scheme with a graph convolutional network that leverages the circuit dynamics' coupling structure. The learned parameters are constrained to the ranges required by the theoretical stability conditions. Numerical experiments demonstrate the rapid suppression of chaotic oscillations and the finite-time convergence of the state and sliding variables while respecting the prescribed input-saturation limits. Compared to a reference control method, the proposed approach reduces the IAE, ISE, ITAE, and ITSE by 56.27\,\%, 56.40\,\%, 61.69\,\%, and 64.70\,\%, respectively. These results suggest that graph-based reinforcement learning can effectively tune parameters while retaining the robustness and analytical structure of the underlying sliding-mode controller.
