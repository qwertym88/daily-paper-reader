## 摘要
我们研究具有若干复合不等式约束的凸差优化问题，并提出一种主化引导的切换近端梯度（MGSPG）方法。具体而言，在每个可行外点处，二次主化与线性化产生一个强凸复合代理问题。为近似求解该代理问题，我们采用切换近端梯度（SPG）方法：当当前点近似可行时，对目标函数采取近端梯度步；否则，对违反程度最大的约束采取近端梯度步。该构造既不需要投影到联合代理可行集上，也不需要计算乘子。通过建立一个公共不变球并利用权重递增论证，我们得到 SPG 的 O(τ^{-1}) 内层复杂度界。基于该界，我们证明：在可行水平集有界以及代理问题满足一致 Slater 条件的假设下，MGSPG 能在 O(ε^{-2}) 次外层迭代和 O(ε^{-4}) 次选定分量的近端梯度更新内找到一个 ε-KKT 点。在 SCAD 约束和组约束回归问题上的数值实验表明，与现有优化算法相比，我们的方法具有有效性和计算效率。

## Abstract
We study difference-of-convex optimization problems with several composite inequality constraints and develop a majorization-guided switching proximal gradient (MGSPG) method. Specifically, at each feasible outer point, quadratic majorization and linearization yield a strongly convex composite surrogate problem. To solve this surrogate approximately, we employ a switching proximal gradient (SPG) method, which takes a proximal gradient step for the objective when the current point is nearly feasible and for a most violated constraint otherwise. This construction requires neither projection onto the joint surrogate feasible set nor multiplier computation. By establishing a common invariant ball and using an increasing weight argument, we obtain an \(O(τ^{-1})\) inner complexity bound for SPG. Building on this bound, we show that, under boundedness of the feasible level set and a uniform Slater condition for the surrogates, MGSPG finds an \(\varepsilon\)-KKT point in \(O(\varepsilon^{-2})\) outer iterations and \(O(\varepsilon^{-4})\) selected component proximal gradient updates. Numerical experiments on SCAD-constrained and groupwise-constrained regression problems demonstrate the viability and computational efficiency of our method compared with existing optimization algorithms.
