## 摘要
信号时序逻辑（STL）可指定具有时空要求的机器人任务，其鲁棒语义允许通过轨迹优化进行规划。在实际中，机器人从含噪测量中估计状态；当测量所获得的信息依赖于状态时，机器人可以通过在信念空间中进行规划来主动降低其不确定性。然而，由此产生的问题通常是非凸的，基于梯度的方法对此既不完备也不全局最优。本文考虑高斯信念空间中的风险STL（RiSTL）规划，并表明信念的信息形式参数化结合信念动力学的凸松弛可产生混合整数凸规划（MICP）。我们证明该MICP是可靠且完备的，并且该松弛保持了全局最优输入轨迹。我们通过数值仿真验证了方法的有效性。

## Abstract
Signal Temporal Logic (STL) specifies robot tasks with spatio-temporal requirements, and its robustness semantics allow planning by trajectory optimization. In practice, robots estimate their state from noisy measurements, and when the information gained from a measurement depends on the state, the robot can actively reduce its uncertainty by planning in the belief space. The resulting problem is, however, generally non-convex, for which gradient-based methods are neither complete nor globally optimal. In this paper, we consider Risk STL (RiSTL) planning in Gaussian belief space and show that an information-form parameterization of the belief, together with a convex relaxation of the belief dynamics, yields a mixed-integer convex program (MICP). We prove that the MICP is sound and complete, and that the relaxation preserves the globally optimal input trajectories. We validate the effectiveness of our method in numerical simulations.
