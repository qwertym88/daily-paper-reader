## 摘要
马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）的安全性验证旨在询问：在每个调度器下，到达若干失效状态的概率是否共同位于一个凸安全区域内。Watanabe 与 Unno（2026）通过细化失效概率的外近似来证明安全性，其中每次查询都会对概率的一个非负加权和进行约束。他们的规则依据最大欧几里得违反量来选择权重。我们提出了一个统一框架，以显式选定的误差单位来度量违反量：方向得分由外近似与安全区域之间的支撑函数差，并按该单位的支撑函数归一化后给出。我们证明，当最大得分为正时，它等于以该单位为倍数、包含该外近似的最小安全区域扩大倍数。欧几里得单位可恢复先前的规则；单位立方体与安全区域本身则生成两条新规则。我们通过实验评估测量规则的选择如何影响 MDP 安全性验证的性能。

## Abstract
Safety verification of a Markov decision process (MDP) asks whether the probabilities of reaching several failure states jointly lie in a convex safe region under every scheduler. Watanabe and Unno (2026) prove safety by refining an outer approximation of the failure probabilities, where each query bounds a nonnegative weighted sum of the probabilities. Their rule selects weights by the largest Euclidean violation. We present a unified framework to measure violation in an explicitly chosen error unit: directions are scored by the support-function gap between the outer approximation and the safe region, normalized by the support function of the unit. We show that the maximal score, when positive, equals the smallest enlargement of the safe region in multiples of that unit that contains the outer approximation. The Euclidean unit recovers the earlier rule; two new rules are generated by the unit cube and the safe region itself. We experimentally evaluate how the choice of measurement rule affects the performance of safety verification for MDPs.
