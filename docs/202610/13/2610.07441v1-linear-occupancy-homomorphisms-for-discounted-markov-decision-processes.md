## 摘要
我们引入线性占用同态（LOHs），这是一类马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）变换，它在 MDP 的可行占用度量空间上定义 MDP 同态。与通过状态和状态-动作空间上的映射来定义的经典 MDP 同态框架不同，LOH 在占用度量空间之间进行映射，并且可表示为线性变换矩阵，从而揭示同态本身的重要结构性质。对于这类 MDP 变换，我们推导出有限维线性不等式约束，用于判定候选变换矩阵是否为有效的 LOH，并推导出相应的变换后 MDP。我们进一步建立 LOH、软 MDP 同态与因子化矩阵鲁棒 MDP 之间的联系。当线性变换矩阵可逆时，我们证明原 MDP 在同态变换后等价于一个约束 MDP，并推导出 LOH 保持最优值的充要条件。我们在两种情形中说明 LOH 的优势：通过将非矩形参数不确定性集变换为同态的矩形 MDP 来改进鲁棒 MDP 解，以及将一个大 MDP 分解为较小的独立 MDP。

## Abstract
We introduce linear occupancy homomorphisms (LOHs), a class of Markov decision process (MDP) transformations that defines the MDP homomorphism over the feasible occupancy measure space of MDPs. In contrast to classic MDP homomorphism frameworks, which are defined through mappings over the state and state-action spaces, LOHs map between occupancy measure spaces and are representable as linear transformation matrices that expose important structural properties of the homomorphism itself. For this class of MDP transformations, we derive finite dimensional linear inequality constraints that certify when a candidate transformation matrix is a valid LOH and derive the corresponding transformed MDP. We further establish a connection between LOHs, soft MDP homomorphisms, and factored matrix robust MDPs. When the linear transformation matrix is invertible, we prove that the original MDP is equivalent to a constrained MDP after the homomorphic transformation, and derive necessary and sufficient conditions under which the LOH preserves the optimal value. We illustrate the benefits of LOHs in two settings: improving robust MDP solutions by transforming nonrectangular parameter uncertainty sets into homomorphic rectangular MDPs, and decomposing a large MDP into smaller independent MDPs.
