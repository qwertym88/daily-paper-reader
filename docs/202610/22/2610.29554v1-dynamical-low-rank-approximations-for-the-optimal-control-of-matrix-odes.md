## 摘要
我们考虑一类动力系统的开环最优控制，其状态由时间依赖矩阵 X(t)∈R^{m×n} 描述。基于梯度的优化方法可能带来可观的计算与存储开销，尤其对于大规模系统和精细时间离散化。为减轻这一计算负担，我们建立了一个数学框架，在最优控制问题中使用动态低秩近似（DLRA）。我们研究两种不同的范式：第一种称为先优化后投影（Optimize-then-Project, OtP），先推导最优性系统，然后利用 DLRA 同时近似状态方程和伴随方程。第二种称为先投影后优化（Project-then-Optimize, PtO），先以状态方程的 DLR 近似替换状态方程，然后推导所得优化问题的最优性条件。这两种方法在伴随变量的作用方面存在根本差异。在 OtP 中，伴随变量在固定秩矩阵流形上演化；而在 PtO 中，伴随变量属于演化状态变量处固定秩流形的切空间。后一种表述导出一个协变常微分方程，用于描述当底层切空间随时间演化时伴随变量的动力学。对这两种方法，我们为近似梯度建立了严格的误差估计，并识别其精度如何依赖于初始和终端条件的低秩近似、状态与伴随向量场在相关切空间法向的分量、动力系统的稳定性性质以及流形曲率。PtO 还需要为伴随协变常微分方程设计定制的时间离散策略；我们证明这些策略对状态变量的小奇异值是稳健的。为说明该框架，我们将这些方法应用于一个随机抛物型偏微分方程的最优控制。

## Abstract
We consider the open-loop optimal control of a dynamical system whose state is described by a time-dependent matrix $X(t)\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$. Gradient-based optimization methods may incur substantial computational and memory costs, particularly for large-scale systems and fine temporal discretizations. To alleviate this computational burden, we develop a mathematical framework to use Dynamical Low Rank Approximations (DLRA) in optimal control problems. We study two distinct paradigms: the first one, called Optimize-then-Project (OtP), first derives the optimality system and then approximates both the state and adjoint equations using DLRA. The second one, called Project-then-Optimize (PtO), first replaces the state equation with its DLR approximation, and then derives the optimality conditions for the resulting optimization problem. The two approaches differ fundamentally concerning the role of the adjoint variable. In OtP, the adjoint variable evolves on the manifold of fixed rank matrices, whereas in PtO, the adjoint variable belongs to the tangent space to the fixed-rank manifold at the evolving state variable. This latter formulation leads to a covariant ODE describing the dynamics of the adjoint variable as the underlying tangent space evolves in time. For both approaches we establish rigorous error estimates for the approximated gradients, identifying how their accuracy depends on the low-rank approximations of initial and terminal conditions, the components of the state and adjoint vector fields normal to the relevant tangent spaces, the stability properties of the dynamics, and the manifold curvature. PtO further requires tailored time discretization strategies for the adjoint covariant ODE, which we show to be robust against small singular values of the state variable. To illustrate the framework, we apply these methods to the optimal control of a random parabolic PDE.
