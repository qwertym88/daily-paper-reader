## 摘要
近期工作已证明，当残差分支按 $1/\sqrt{depth}$ 缩放时，残差网络的无穷宽极限与无穷深极限在初始化时可交换。特别地，无论宽度和深度是依次趋于无穷还是联合趋于无穷，网络输出都收敛到同一极限。我们将这一可交换性结果推广到线性残差网络的训练中。在相同的 $1/\sqrt{depth}$ 残差分支缩放（Depth-μP）和 μP 宽度参数化下，我们证明：对于任意固定数量的带梯度裁剪的梯度下降步，当宽度和深度均趋于无穷时，网络输出沿每一条宽度—深度联合增长路径都在 $L^2$ 中收敛。因此，在任意有限训练时域内，无穷宽极限与无穷深极限可交换。若无梯度裁剪，相应的 $L^2$ 收敛一般不再成立，但我们建立了较弱的依概率收敛。

## Abstract
Recent work established that the infinite-width and infinite-depth limits of residual networks commute at initialization when residual branches are scaled by $1/\sqrt{depth}$. In particular, the network output converges to the same limit whether width and depth tend to infinity sequentially or jointly. We extend this commutativity result to training in linear residual networks. Under the same $1/\sqrt{depth}$ residual-branch scaling (Depth-$μ$P) and the $μ$P width parameterization, we prove that, for any fixed number of gradient-descent steps with gradient clipping, the network output converges in $L^2$ along every joint width--depth growth path as both width and depth tend to infinity. Consequently, the infinite-width and infinite-depth limits commute throughout any finite training horizon. Without gradient clipping, the corresponding $L^2$ convergence does not hold in general, but we establish the weaker convergence in probability.
