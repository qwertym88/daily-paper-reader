## 摘要
标量测量可以在不辨识潜在协方差或每个决策风险的情况下确定一个鲁棒决策。我们研究具有固定均值加标准差目标的有限高斯投影实验。观测律定义了一组不可区分的矩模型。我们展示了一些决策，它们尽管风险值未辨识，却在该集合中处处一致；并给出一个置信集证书，它返回一个决策或一个未解决结果。我们证明了可靠性、对于具有不相容最优值的不可区分模型必须弃权，以及固定检查点的认证保证。一个标量剖面将非线性风险比较约化为凸内层问题。在七个合成实例中，联合校准的矩区域给出的充分预算为显式期望样本信息下界的21至52倍。单独测量的停止时间更低，在1,900条模拟流中决策错误为零。这些比较不构成匹配界，且这些保证要求高斯复制。

## Abstract
A scalar measurement can determine a robust decision without identifying the latent covariance or every decision risk. We study finite Gaussian projection experiments with a fixed mean-plus-standard-deviation target. The observation laws define a set of indistinguishable moment models. We exhibit decisions that agree throughout that set despite unidentified risk values, and give a confidence-set certificate that returns a decision or an unresolved outcome. We prove soundness, necessary abstention for indistinguishable models with incompatible optima, and a fixed-checkpoint certification guarantee. A scalar profile reduces nonlinear risk comparisons to convex inner problems. Across seven synthetic instances, jointly calibrated moment regions give sufficient budgets 21 to 52 times explicit expected-sample information lower bounds. Separately measured stopping times are lower, with zero decision errors in 1,900 simulated streams. These comparisons do not constitute matching bounds, and the guarantees require Gaussian replication.
