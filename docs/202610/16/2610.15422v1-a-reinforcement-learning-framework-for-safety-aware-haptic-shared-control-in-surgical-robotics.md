## 摘要
手术机器人中的触觉共享控制在共享末端执行器处融合来自人类侧和机器人侧的力，同时保持操作者的参与。然而，几何安全选择对任务执行与力协调的影响仍不明确。我们将这种交互建模为集中式训练与分散式执行下的双智能体合作马尔可夫博弈，并将学习得到的人类控制与任务调优的固定阻抗基线进行比较。该无模型多智能体强化学习框架支持联合策略自适应，而无需完整的解析交互模型。它在常见几何任务定义下，将 MADDPG 和 rMAPPO 与一种 epigraph 约束方法进行比较。在仿真中，rMAPPO 通常取得最高的回报和跟踪性能，而 epigraph 方法产生更保守的运动和更低的几何暴露，包括在几何迁移后未观察到碰撞或危险区域暴露。在真实操作者输入下，固定阻抗训练得到的策略更快，并在所有记录试验中均成功。与学习到的伙伴一起训练的策略表现出更大的指令对抗性，而固定 epigraph 条件产生对齐程度最高的指令。这项工作为研究触觉共享控制中的人类控制表示与约束处理权衡提供了一个基准。

## Abstract
Haptic shared control in surgical robotics combines human-side and robot-side forces at a shared end effector while preserving operator involvement. However, the effects of geometric safety choices on task execution and force coordination remain unclear. We model the interaction as a two-agent cooperative Markov game under centralized training and decentralized execution, comparing learned human control with a task-tuned fixed-impedance baseline. This model-free multi-agent reinforcement learning framework supports joint policy adaptation without requiring a complete analytical interaction model. It compares MADDPG and rMAPPO with an epigraph-constrained approach under common geometric task definitions. In simulation, rMAPPO generally achieved the highest return and tracking performance, whereas the epigraph approach produced more conservative motions and lower geometric exposure, including no observed collision or dangerous-region exposure after geometry transfer. With real operator input, fixed-impedance-trained policies were faster and successful in all recorded trials. Policies trained with a learned partner exhibited greater command opposition, while the fixed epigraph condition produced the most aligned commands. This work provides a benchmark for studying human-control representation and constraint-handling trade-offs in haptic shared control.
