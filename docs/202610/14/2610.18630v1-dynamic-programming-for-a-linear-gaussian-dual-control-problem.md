## 摘要
我们考虑一个针对可数新息信念状态模型的无限时域折扣动态规划（DP）问题。该模型是涉及未知参数向量和高斯观测噪声的线性高斯对偶控制问题的一个替代模型。该替代模型保留了高斯新息的前两阶矩以及由此得到的后验二阶矩结构。我们允许无界的信念空间和控制空间，以及在后验均值和协方差上具有二次增长的非负阶段成本。利用后验矩权重，我们证明贝尔曼算子在加权上确界范数空间中是一个压缩算子。我们刻画最优成本为该空间中贝尔曼方程的唯一解，并建立最优平稳策略的存在性。我们进一步证明在近似误差消失时，值迭代、策略迭代和乐观策略迭代的加权上确界范数收敛性。近似误差消失下的收敛结果依赖于一般的加权上确界范数压缩性质，并且适用于本文所考虑的对偶控制问题之外的更一般情形。

## Abstract
We consider an infinite-horizon discounted dynamic programming (DP) problem for a countable-innovation belief-state model. The model is a surrogate for a linear-Gaussian dual control problem involving an unknown parameter vector and Gaussian observation noise. The surrogate preserves the first two moments of the Gaussian innovation and the resulting posterior second-moment structure. We allow unbounded belief and control spaces and nonnegative stage costs with quadratic growth in the posterior mean and covariance. Using a posterior-moment weight, we show that the Bellman operator is a contraction in a weighted sup-norm space. We characterize the optimal cost as the unique solution of the Bellman equation in this space, establish the existence of optimal stationary policies. We further prove weighted sup-norm convergence of value iteration, policy iteration, and optimistic policy iteration under vanishing approximation errors. The convergence results under vanishing approximation errors rely on general weighted sup-norm contraction properties and are applicable beyond the particular dual control problem considered here.
