## 摘要
本文从控制的视角概述了深度学习的最新进展。文章回顾了几类模型，包括前馈神经网络和残差神经网络、Transformer、大语言模型以及选择性状态空间模型，同时解释了控制理论如何在理解它们的训练、性质或架构设计中发挥重要作用。文章说明了残差神经网络如何可以被解释为连续时间控制系统的离散化，从而能够利用控制理论技术对其进行分析。为说明控制与深度学习之间这一桥梁的强大作用，文中讨论了两个技术结果：1）残差神经网络逼近任意连续函数的能力是一个非标准可控性问题，可以使用几何非线性控制来求解；2）Transformer 是大多数大语言模型的关键架构组件，可以看作一致性算法的离散化，因此大语言模型表现出一致性。控制理论与深度学习之间的另一个接触点是反向传播——用于训练神经网络的算法——与最优控制之间的联系。文章讨论了这些问题之间较早的联系（20世纪80年代使用离散时间模型建立）如何与较新的使用连续时间模型的方法相协调。文章还讨论了若干开放问题和未来研究方向，包括选择性状态空间模型，这是一类受控制中所用模型启发而近期出现的模型。

## Abstract
This article offers an overview of recent developments in deep learning from a control vantage point. It reviews several classes of models including feedforward and residual neural networks, transformers, large-language models, and selective state space models while explaining how control theory plays an important role in understanding their training, properties, or architecture design. The article explains how residual neural networks can be interpreted as the discretization of a continuous-time control system thereby enabling the use of control theoretic techniques for its analysis. To illustrate the power of this bridge between control and deep learning, two technical results are discussed: 1) the ability of a residual neural network to approximate arbitrary continuous functions is a non-standard controllability problem that can be solved using geometric nonlinear control and 2) transformers, the key architectural component of most large language-models, can be seen the discretization of a consensus algorithm and thus large language-models exhibit consensus. Another point of contact between control theory and deep learning is the connection between backpropagation, the algorithm used to train neural networks, and optimal control. The article addresses how the older connection between these problems, established in the 80's using discrete-time models can be reconciled with the more recent approach using continuous-time models. Several open problems and directions for future research are discussed, including selective state-space models, a recent class of models inspired by the models used in control.

### 2.2 关键技术细节

- **固定输入情形**：当控制输入 `W_c` 已知且固定时，ResNet对微分方程的逼近是一个经典数值分析问题，在Σ连续可微、`W_c` 有界等条件下，存在 `ℓ` 和 `w` 使逼近误差 `ε` 任意小。
