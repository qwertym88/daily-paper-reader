## 摘要
人形机器人全身遥操作将人体运动实时转化为稳定的机器人行为。现有系统通常依赖在线运动重定向来弥合人-机器人形态差异，但这一过程会增加延迟，并可能产生物理上不可行的目标。与此同时，多样化、含噪且部分可观测的人体运动观测往往落在训练分布之外，可能导致机器人行为不稳定。我们提出一种免重定向策略，可在单次前向传播中将原始人体运动直接映射为机器人关节指令，从而消除在线运动学适应。为提高鲁棒性，我们学习了一个全身运动基元码本，其可将分布外观测投影到合理的运动原型上，并从部分输入中恢复全身运动。在 Unitree G1 上进行的仿真与硬件实验，使用虚拟现实、光学动作捕捉、文本到运动生成以及单目视频输入，表明我们的方法在延迟和鲁棒性方面优于基线。

## Abstract
Humanoid whole-body teleoperation translates human motion into stable robot behavior in real time. Existing systems typically rely on online motion retargeting to bridge human--robot morphological differences, but this process adds latency and can produce physically infeasible targets. Meanwhile, diverse, noisy, and partial human-motion observations often fall outside the training distribution, potentially causing unstable robot behavior. We propose a retargeting-free policy that maps raw human motion directly to robot joint commands in a single forward pass, eliminating online kinematic adaptation. To improve robustness, we learn a codebook of full-body motion primitives that projects out-of-distribution observations onto plausible motion prototypes and recovers full-body motion from partial inputs. Experiments on a Unitree~G1 in simulation and on hardware, using virtual reality, optical mocap, text-to-motion generation, and monocular video inputs, show that our method outperforms baselines in latency and robustness.
