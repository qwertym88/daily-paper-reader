## 摘要
我们研究具有马尔可夫机制转换的连续时间随机控制中的策略评估（PE）与策略梯度（PG）。主要挑战在于扩散与机制跳跃共存、依赖于机制的值函数之间的耦合，以及由耦合生成元构造无模型学习规则。我们建立了同时包含布朗鞅和切换鞅的PE鞅刻画，并将鞅损失与正交性方法推广到该设定。进一步推导了耦合PG方程和基于轨迹的梯度表示；在切换生成元与动作无关的条件下，该表示避免了对系统系数和转移率的显式知识。这些结果导出了离线与在线actor-critic算法。我们还建立了收敛性，并研究了实现所需的有限网格近似。针对机制转换线性二次问题的数值实验验证了所提方法。

## Abstract
We study policy evaluation (PE) and policy gradient (PG) for continuous-time stochastic control with Markov regime-switching. The main challenges are the coexistence of diffusion and regime jumps, the coupling of regime-dependent value functions, and the construction of model-free learning rules from the coupled generators. We establish a martingale characterization of PE that incorporates both Brownian and switching martingales, and extend martingale loss and orthogonality methods to this setting. We further derive a coupled PG equation and a trajectory-based gradient representation that, under an action-independent switching generator, avoids explicit knowledge of the system coefficients and transition rates. These results lead to offline and online actor-critic algorithms. We also establish convergence and study the finite-grid approximation required for implementation. Numerical experiments for a regime-switching linear-quadratic problem illustrate the proposed methods.
