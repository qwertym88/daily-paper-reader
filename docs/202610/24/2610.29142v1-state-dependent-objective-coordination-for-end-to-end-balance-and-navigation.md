## 摘要
传统机器人导航系统通过级联模块将建图、规划与底层控制分离，带来计算开销和累积时延。端到端强化学习提供了一种紧凑的统一策略，可将车载观测直接映射为控制动作。然而，在两轮倒立摆机器人等本质上不稳定的平台上，导航进展与避障会以强状态依赖的方式与姿态稳定发生冲突。我们提出一种轻量级的状态依赖目标协调方法，用于集成导航与控制。一个以俯仰角为条件的标量化系数 β(s)∈[0,1] 在训练期间调节平衡稳定与导航目标的相对贡献，无需额外控制层级或辅助姿态稳定控制器（如比例-积分-微分（PID）或线性二次调节器（LQR）控制器）。为支持零样本部署，仿真训练对执行延迟、动作保持、异步且含噪观测以及动力学不确定性进行建模。梯度分析揭示了持续存在的方向性干扰以及目标相对尺度随状态变化的现象。与替代的奖励加权、约束、训练和控制结构策略相比，PPO+β(s)（PPO 表示近端策略优化）在固定障碍地图和随机生成的高障碍密度地图上分别取得 98% 和 95% 的成功率，且姿态偏差较小。零样本硬件实验进一步表明，仅使用车载观测即可实现有界姿态行为和一致的避障。训练与评估代码将在论文被接收后公开。

## Abstract
Traditional robotic navigation systems separate mapping, planning, and low-level control through cascaded modules, introducing computational overhead and accumulated latency. End-to-end reinforcement learning offers a compact unified policy that maps onboard observations directly to control actions. However, on inherently unstable platforms such as two-wheeled inverted-pendulum robots, navigation progress and obstacle avoidance can conflict with attitude stabilization in a strongly state-dependent manner. We propose a lightweight state-dependent objective coordination method for integrated navigation and control. A pitch-conditioned scalarization coefficient $β(s)\in[0,1]$ adjusts the relative contributions of balance stabilization and navigation objectives during training, without extra control hierarchies or auxiliary attitude-stabilization controllers, such as proportional-integral-derivative (PID) or linear-quadratic regulator (LQR) controllers. To support zero-shot deployment, simulation training models execution delay, action hold, asynchronous and noisy observations, and dynamics uncertainty. Gradient analysis reveals persistent directional interference and state-dependent variation in the objectives' relative scales. Comparisons with alternative reward-weighting, constraint, training, and control-structure strategies show that PPO+$β(s)$, where PPO denotes proximal policy optimization, achieves 98% and 95% success rates with small attitude excursions on the fixed obstacle map and the randomly generated high-obstacle-density map, respectively. Zero-shot hardware experiments further demonstrate bounded attitude behavior and consistent obstacle avoidance using only onboard observations. Training and evaluation code will be made publicly available upon acceptance.
