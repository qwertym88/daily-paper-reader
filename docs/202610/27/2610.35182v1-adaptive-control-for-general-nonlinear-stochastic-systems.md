## 摘要
本文将经典线性自校正调节器的最小二乘与确定性等价最小方差框架扩展到具有紧参数空间的一大类非线性参数化系统，包括常用的深度神经网络模型。首先，在一般连续性模下，将历史非线性最小二乘拟合保证转化为在线一步预测界，并在参数 Lipschitz 连续与有限矩噪声条件下给出显式遗憾率。其次，近似 NLS 估计结合确定性等价控制可在温和条件下实现闭环稳定性和渐近最优跟踪，而无需激励或参数可辨识性。在参数 Lipschitz 情形下，建立了显式闭环遗憾率和跟踪误差率；闭环例子表明，对于该调节器，这些遗憾率一般无法改进。最后，通过证明闭环稳定性得以保持，且跟踪误差界由一个消失项和一个由模型差异决定的残差项组成，建立了对小的未建模动态的鲁棒性。

## Abstract
This paper extends the least-squares and certainty-equivalence minimum-variance framework of classical linear self-tuning regulators to a broad class of nonlinearly parameterized systems with compact parameter spaces, including commonly used deep neural network models. First, historical nonlinear least-squares fitting guarantees are converted into online one-step prediction bounds under a general modulus of continuity, with explicit regret rates under parameter Lipschitz continuity and finite-moment noise. Second, approximate NLS estimation combined with certainty-equivalence control achieves closed-loop stability and asymptotically optimal tracking under mild conditions without requiring excitation or parameter identifiability. Explicit closed-loop regret and tracking error rates are established in the parameter-Lipschitz case, and closed-loop examples show that the regret rates cannot, in general, be improved for this regulator. Finally, robustness to small unmodeled dynamics is established by showing that closed-loop stability is preserved and the tracking error bounds comprise a vanishing term and a residual term determined by the model discrepancy.
