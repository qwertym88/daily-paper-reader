## 摘要
我们研究Nesterov加速梯度下降（NAG）方法在确定性和随机设置下用于凸优化的收敛速率。我们关注由若干机器学习问题在经验和理论上提出的一种更一般的光滑性条件。我们证明，在可获得目标函数精确梯度的条件下，就函数值间隙而言，其加速收敛速率为 \mathcal{O}\left(1/T^2\right)，与 \citep{nesterov1983method} 中标准光滑凸优化的最优速率相匹配。在随机优化的松弛仿射方差噪声假设下，我们建立了阶为 \tilde{\mathcal{O}}\left(\sqrt{\log\left(1/δ\right)/T}\right) 的高概率收敛速率，并且当噪声参数足够小时，该速率可改进至 \tilde{\mathcal{O}}\left(\log\left(1/δ\right)/T^2\right)。其中，T 表示总迭代次数，δ 为概率裕度。在相差对数因子的意义下，我们的概率收敛速率达到了与 \citep{ghadimi2016accelerated} 中所获期望速率相同的阶；后者需要有限方差噪声和Lipschitz光滑性假设。我们额外建立了所研究算法在非凸设置下的收敛速率。

## Abstract
We study convergence rates of Nesterov's Accelerated Gradient Descent (NAG) method for convex optimization in both deterministic and stochastic settings. We focus on a more general smoothness condition raised from several machine learning problems empirically and theoretically. We show the accelerated convergence rate of order $\mathcal{O}\left(1/T^2\right)$ in terms of the function value gap, given access to exact gradients of objective functions, matching the optimal rate for standard smooth convex optimization in \citep{nesterov1983method}. Under the relaxed affine-variance noise assumption for stochastic optimization, we establish the high-probability convergence rate of order $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}\left(\sqrt{\log\left(1/δ\right)/T}\right)$ and this rate could improve to $\tilde{\mathcal{O}}\left(\log\left(1/δ\right)/T^2\right)$ when the noise parameters are sufficiently small. Here, $T$ denotes the total number of iterations and $δ$ is the probability margin. Up to logarithm factors, our probabilistic convergence rate reaches the same order of the expected rate obtained in \citep{ghadimi2016accelerated} where the assumptions of bounded variance noise and Lipschitz smoothness are required. We additionally establish the convergence rates of the studied algorithms in the non-convex setting.
