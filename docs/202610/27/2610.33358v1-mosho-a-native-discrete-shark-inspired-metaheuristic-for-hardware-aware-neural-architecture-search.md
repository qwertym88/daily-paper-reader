## 摘要
硬件感知神经架构搜索（NAS）需要在离散搜索空间上联合优化预测精度与设备特定延迟。尽管多目标群智能优化器展现出潜力，但多目标白鲨优化器（MOWSO）等连续形式在舍入为整数编码架构向量时可能受到量化噪声影响。为解决这一局限，我们提出 MOSHO（多目标鲨鱼狩猎优化），一种原生离散元启发式方法，用离散架构编辑算子替代连续运动方程。MOSHO 集成了自适应算子调度、能量调节重启和延迟拥挤距离截断，以增强探索能力与搜索效率。在涵盖 18 个上下文的合并 NAS-Bench-201/HW-NAS-Bench 基准上，MOSHO 取得 0.9987 的中位归一化超体积和 0.0064 的 IGD+，其 Pareto 前沿与 NSGA-II 具有竞争力，同时将搜索运行时间减少 96.4%（0.64 秒 vs. 17.72 秒）。这些结果表明，定制离散搜索机制能够使群智能优化器在硬件感知 NAS 中以显著更低的搜索成本实现高质量的多目标解。

## Abstract
Hardware-aware neural architecture search (NAS) requires jointly optimizing predictive accuracy and device-specific latency over discrete search spaces. While multi-objective swarm optimizers show promise, continuous formulations such as the Multi-Objective White Shark Optimizer (MOWSO) can suffer from quantization noise when rounded to integer-encoded architecture vectors. To address this limitation, we propose MOSHO (Multi-Objective Shark Hunting Optimization), a native discrete metaheuristic that replaces continuous motion equations with discrete architecture-editing operators. MOSHO integrates adaptive operator scheduling, energy-regulated restarts, and deferred crowding-distance truncation to enhance exploration and search efficiency. On the merged NAS-Bench-201/HW-NAS-Bench benchmark across 18 contexts, MOSHO achieves a median normalized hypervolume of 0.9987 and IGD+ of 0.0064, yielding Pareto fronts competitive with NSGA-II while reducing search runtime by 96.4% (0.64 s vs. 17.72 s). These results demonstrate that tailored discrete search mechanisms can enable swarm optimizers to achieve high-quality multi-objective solution quality with substantially lower search cost in hardware-aware NAS.
