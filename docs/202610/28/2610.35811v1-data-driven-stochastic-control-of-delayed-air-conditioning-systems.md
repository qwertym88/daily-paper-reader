## 摘要
我们考虑一类源于具有延迟启动效应的工业空调系统运行中的随机最优控制问题。非运行与运行状态之间的延迟切换在被控动力学中引入了非马尔可夫结构。我们在嵌套的状态-控制网格上推导了一种基于回归的离散动态规划算法。我们给出了理想化时间离散动态规划问题收敛到连续时间值函数的概率证明。基于校准的多维预测跟踪 Ornstein--Uhlenbeck 模型的数值实验说明了所提方法的有效性。

## Abstract
We consider a stochastic optimal control problem arising in the operation of industrial air-conditioning systems with delayed activation effects. The delayed transition between inactive and operational regimes induces a non-Markovian structure in the controlled dynamics. We derive a regression-based discrete dynamic programming algorithm on nested state-control grids. We give a probabilistic proof of convergence of the idealized time-discrete dynamic programming problem to the continuous-time value function. Numerical experiments based on calibrated multidimensional forecast-tracking Ornstein--Uhlenbeck models illustrate the effectiveness of the proposed methodology.
