## 摘要
物理神经网络（PNNs）利用电子学、光学及相关领域中的器件物理，实现高能效神经网络。破同构（Broken-isomorphism）PNNs 用多变量物理变换替代线性运算，改变输入信号映射到器件变量的方式。我们通过将信号与物理器件输入变量之间的路由视为可训练参数，来利用这些映射所产生的网络架构。该方法扩展了物理变换在器件层面的可调性，同时在系统层面以可忽略的额外推理开销展示了 PNNs 表达能力的提升。这种离散路由通过使用 Gumbel-Softmax 重参数化训练分类分布来优化。我们使用针对 MNIST 和 CIFAR-10 分类的多变量电子 RNPU 的实验校准可微模型来评估该方法。利用变量选择，我们将小型 CNN 模型的 MNIST 分类准确率从 94.5% 提升到 96.9%，并将 CIFAR-10 的准确率从 62.8% 提升到 68.0%。我们的方法独特地利用了破同构 PNN 架构所诱导的偏置，为更高效的基于物理的神经网络推理铺平了道路。

## Abstract
Physical neural networks (PNNs) leverage device physics in electronics, optics, and related domains to enable energy-efficient neural networks. Broken-isomorphism PNNs replace linear operations with multivariate physical transformations, modifying how input signals are mapped to device variables. We exploit network architectures emerging from these mappings by treating the routing between signals and input variables of physical devices as trainable parameters. This approach expands device-level tunability of the physical transformations while, at the system level, demonstrating improved expressivity of PNNs with negligible additional inference overhead. This discrete routing is optimized by training a categorical distribution using Gumbel-Softmax reparametrization. We evaluate this approach using experimentally calibrated differentiable models of multivariate electronic RNPUs for MNIST and CIFAR-10 classification. Using variable selection, we improve the MNIST classification accuracy for a small CNN model from 94.5% to 96.9% and the accuracy of CIFAR-10 from 62.8% to 68.0%. Our method uniquely leverages the induced biases of broken-isomorphism PNN architectures, paving the way towards more efficient physics-based neural network inference.
