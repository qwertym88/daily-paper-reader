## 摘要
开环最小能量控制（MEC）问题要求控制输入在使终端状态达到期望值的同时最小化控制能量。本文研究随机线性时不变系统的开环 MEC 问题。我们考虑多种强制终端误差“较小”的随机约束，并表明随机 MEC（SMEC）问题可以表述为一个 QCQP。我们得到了 SMEC 的最优解，并表明它通过引入可控性和 Gramian 矩阵的新型随机变体，推广了著名的确定性 MEC 问题。最后，我们表明最优控制能量存在一个基本下界，该下界无法通过增加问题的时间范围来克服。

## Abstract
The open-loop minimum-energy control (MEC) problem asks for control inputs that minimize the control energy while ensuring the terminal state reaches a desired value. In this paper, we study the open-loop MEC problem for stochastic linear time-invariant systems. We consider a variety of stochastic constraints that enforce the terminal error to be \emph{small}, and show that Stochastic MEC (SMEC) problem can be formulated as a QCQP. We obtain the optimal solution for SMEC and show that it generalizes the well-known Deterministic MEC problem by introducing novel stochastic variants of controllability and Gramian matrices. Finally, we show that there exists a fundamental lower bound on the optimal control energy which cannot be overcome by increasing the time horizon of the problem.
