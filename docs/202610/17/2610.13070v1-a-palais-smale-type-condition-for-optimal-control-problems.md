## 摘要
我们考虑一个具有一般控制约束和固定初始状态的最优控制问题，其中不存在凸性假设。为建立最优对的存在性，我们引入一个由临界点理论改编而来的控制版本的 Palais-Smale 型条件，并证明在该条件下最优对存在（见定理 2.1）。随后，我们验证某一类特定的最优控制问题满足我们的 Palais-Smale 型条件（见命题 2.1）。此外，作为一个应用，我们给出一个例子（例 3.1），并表明据我们所知，现有结果均不能用于建立该情形下最优对的存在性（见第 3.1.1 节）。例 3.2 表明，有限多个 Pontryagin 极大值原理候选者并不能保证最优控制的存在。最后，例 3.3 表明，所提出的 Palais-Smale 型条件对于最优控制的存在性是充分的，但不是必要的。

## Abstract
We consider an optimal control problem with a general control constraint and a fixed initial state, for which convexity assumptions are absent. To establish the existence of optimal pairs, we introduce a control version of the Palais-Smale type condition adapted from critical point theory and prove that optimal pairs exist under this condition (see Theorem 2.1). Subsequently, we verify that a specific class of optimal control problems satisfies our Palais-Smale type condition (see Proposition 2.1). Furthermore, as an application, we present an example (Example 3.1) and show that, to the best of our knowledge, no existing result can be applied to establish the existence of an optimal pair in this case (see Section 3.1.1). Example 3.2 shows that finitely many Pontryagin maximum principle candidates do not guarantee the existence of an optimal control. Finally, Example 3.3 demonstrates that the proposed Palais-Smale type condition is sufficient, but not necessary, for the existence of optimal controls.
