## 摘要
我们从相隔固定时间滞后的分子状态对中推断随机反应网络的动力学速率，而无需构建化学主方程的大状态空间。扩展动态模式分解估计分子状态的选定函数的有限时间演化。矩阵对数随后给出一个投影生成元，其物种行通过已知的反应化学计量确定速率。有限函数集引入系统性偏差。我们从反应通道构造附加函数，并证明每一级闭合都将首个偏差项移至更高阶。谱公式识别导致该偏差的遗漏模态，并显示对数何时放大采样误差。精确计算与五个反应网络上的实验证实了预测的误差行为，并表明所添加函数何时改善有限样本下的速率恢复。

## Abstract
We infer kinetic rates of stochastic reaction networks from molecular-state pairs separated by a fixed time lag, without constructing the large state space of the Chemical Master Equation. Extended dynamic mode decomposition estimates the finite-time evolution of selected functions of the molecular state. A matrix logarithm then gives a projected generator whose species rows determine the rates through the known reaction stoichiometry. A finite set of functions introduces a systematic bias. We construct additional functions from the reaction channels and prove that each level of closure moves the first bias term to higher order. Spectral formulas identify the omitted modes responsible for this bias and show when the logarithm amplifies sampling error. Exact calculations and experiments on five reaction networks confirm the predicted error behavior and show when the added functions improve finite-sample rate recovery.
