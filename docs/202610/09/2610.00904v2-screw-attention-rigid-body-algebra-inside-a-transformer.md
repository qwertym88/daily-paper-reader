## 摘要
学习得到的操作策略会从数据中重新发现刚体力学以闭式形式给出的空间关系，这使它们对几何变化十分脆弱。我们提出 Screw Attention，一种 Transformer 层，其中两个物体之间的关系是空间变换而非图边。每个 token 对携带相对位姿，并且对于机器人关节还携带关节旋量。消息沿该关系传输到接收方的坐标系中，而注意力分数只看到坐标系不变的量。由构造可知，消息对每个 token 处独立的坐标系变化具有等变性，且单层即可表达刚体力学的速度递推。在 LIBERO-Spatial 上，仅从物体位姿训练、具有 16k 参数的策略达到 97.3% 成功率，高于同等规模的图网络、Transformer 和扁平网络，以及参数量多 27 倍的扁平网络。消融实验表明，收益来自传输正确的关系，并且该结构在任务需要其他方法无法提供的坐标系间关系时收益最大。这种等变性使策略对机器人的描述方式具有鲁棒性，而其他所有学习网络在坐标系约定变化下都会崩溃。此外，该策略对位姿噪声和校准误差的容忍度至少与解析控制器相当。将其用作解析控制器上的门控残差时，它还能改进接触丰富的插入任务。代码和训练好的策略将会发布。

## Abstract
Learned manipulation policies rediscover from data the spatial relations that rigid-body mechanics supplies in closed form, which leaves them fragile to geometric change. We present Screw Attention, a transformer layer in which the relation between two bodies is a spatial transform rather than a graph edge. Each pair of tokens carries the relative pose and, for robot joints, the joint screw. Messages are transported along this relation into the receiver's frame, while the attention scores see only frame-invariant quantities. By construction, the messages are equivariant to an independent change of frame at every token, and a single layer can express the velocity recursion of rigid-body mechanics. On LIBERO-Spatial, a policy of 16k parameters trained from object poses alone reaches 97.3% success, above graph, transformer and flat networks of the same size and a flat network with 27 times more parameters. Ablations show that the gain comes from transporting the correct relations, and that the structure pays most where the task requires relations between frames that nothing else supplies. The equivariance makes the policy robust to how the robot is described, where every other learned network collapses under a change of frame convention. Furthermore, the policy tolerates pose noise and calibration errors at least as well as an analytic controller. Used as a gated residual on an analytic controller, it also improves a contact-rich insertion task. Code and trained policies will be released.
