## 摘要
我们研究有限时域分幕式马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）中的遗憾最小化。尽管极小极大最优算法已知，但面向实例相关最优性的可处理方法仍然缺乏。受此空白启发，我们提出 π0-PSRL，它是 PSRL 的一种变体，利用后验样本决定何时需要探索，同时在探索回合中遵循固定参考策略 π0。这将探索检验——当采样MDP与经验MDP具有不同最优策略时触发——与用于收集信息的策略选择解耦。由此得到的设计解决了标准 PSRL 的一个局限，即采样得到的最优策略可能并非探索的最优选择。我们证明 π0-PSRL 的实例相关遗憾界，识别出由 π0 诱导的对数探索成本，并朝着匹配分幕式强化学习（RL）的渐近下界迈进一步。我们的证明技术展示了一种新颖的证明结构，用于推导分幕式 MDP 中的问题相关遗憾界，并给出了 Dirichlet 随机变量的集中结果，这些结果可能具有独立意义。

## Abstract
We study regret minimization in finite-horizon episodic Markov Decision Processes (MDPs). While minimax-optimal algorithms are known, tractable approaches to instance-dependent optimality are still lacking. Motivated by this gap, we introduce $π_0$-PSRL, a variant of PSRL that uses posterior samples to decide when exploration is needed, while following a fixed reference policy $π_0$ during exploration episodes. This decouples the test for exploration, triggered when the sampled and empirical MDPs have different optimal policies, from the choice of the policy used to gather information. The resulting design addresses a limitation of standard PSRL, where the sampled optimal policy may not be the optimal choice for exploration. We prove instance-dependent regret bounds for $π_0$-PSRL, identifying the logarithmic exploration cost induced by $π_0$ and taking a step toward matching asymptotic lower bounds for episodic RL. Our proof techniques showcase a novel proof structure to derive problem-dependent regret bounds in episodic MDPs, and concentration results for Dirichlet random variables, that may be of independent interest.
