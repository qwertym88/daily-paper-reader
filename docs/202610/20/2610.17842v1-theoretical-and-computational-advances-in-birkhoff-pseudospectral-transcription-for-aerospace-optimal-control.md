## 摘要
本文针对航空航天最优控制问题，发展了Birkhoff伪谱转录的理论与数值扩展。基于近期提出的知识体系，针对翻转Legendre-Gauss-Radau（fLGR）网格推导了Birkhoff积分算子，并建立了其与常规LGR网格的联系，以及使其Birkhoff插值多项式尤为具有吸引力的良好条件数特性。我们还提出通过伪谱算子施加微分约束，并针对控制速率限制，建立了该方法与状态增广之间的原始-对偶等价性。数值进展包括异构多阶段最优控制问题的建模与求解、参数化非仿射时间映射、仿射缩放与均衡方法的组合，以及将线性伪谱算子与非线性函数数值导数相结合的混合导数策略。这些方法已在RAPTOR（伪谱轨迹优化与鲁棒化快速算法）转录框架中实现，并通过面向特征的基准测试进行评估：一维着陆问题、平面轨道抬升场景、大气进入问题，以及同时包含助推器回收与上面级入轨轨迹的多阶段可重复使用运载器问题。

## Abstract
This paper develops theoretical and numerical extensions of Birkhoff pseudospectral transcription for aerospace optimal control problems. Building on the recently proposed body of knowledge, a Birkhoff integration operator is derived for flipped Legendre Gauss Radau (fLGR) grids, drawing connections to regular LGR grids and to the favorable conditioning properties that make Birkhoff interpolants particularly appealing. We also propose the enforcement of differential constraints via pseudospectral operators, and the primal and dual equivalence of this approach to state augmentation is established for control-rate limits. The numerical developments include the formulation and solution of heterogeneous multiphase optimal control problems, parameterized non-affine time mappings, composition of affine scaling and equilibration methods, and a hybrid derivative strategy to combine linear pseudospectral operators with numerical derivatives for nonlinear functions. These methods are implemented in the RAPTOR (Rapid Algorithm for Pseudospectral Trajectory Optimization and Robustification) transcription framework and assessed through feature-oriented benchmarks: a one-dimensional landing problem, a planar orbit-raising scenario, an atmospheric entry problem, and a multiphase reusable launch vehicle problem with simultaneous booster-recovery and upper-stage-insertion trajectories.
