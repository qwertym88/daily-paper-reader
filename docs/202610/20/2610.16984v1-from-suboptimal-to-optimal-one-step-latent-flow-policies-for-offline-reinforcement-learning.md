## 摘要
离线强化学习旨在不与环境交互的情况下，从固定数据集中学习有效策略。尽管扩散模型和流匹配等生成模型增强了策略的表达能力，但它们通常从无信息的高斯噪声出发，并通过多步迭代生成动作，导致计算成本高，且无法有效利用数据集中的次优轨迹。我们提出 MeanFlow2Better（MF2B），它将离线强化学习重新表述为在高层次潜在决策空间中，从当前性能状态到更优性能状态的生成式决策块优化。MF2B 通过编码器将轨迹块映射为高层次潜在决策表示，使生成起点位于由行为数据塑造的结构化决策空间中。模型在该空间中学习共享的条件平均速度场，构建两种流映射：从高斯诱导的决策源指向正向行为决策区域，以及从次优决策指向高价值决策区域。两种流映射均通过在单位时间区间 $Δt=1$ 上的一步 ODE 积分完成决策细化，既不需要多步迭代采样，也不需要随时间反向传播。此外，我们提出分层块级优势以引导流向高回报区域，其中高层次决策覆盖多个低层动作，从而使序列长度从 $T$ 压缩到 $K \approx T/H$，并显著降低计算开销。在 D4RL Gym-MuJoCo 基准上，MF2B 取得 90.91 的平均得分，达到或超过强基线。

## Abstract
Offline reinforcement learning aims to learn effective policies from fixed datasets without environment interaction. Although generative models such as diffusion and flow matching enhance policy expressiveness, they typically start from uninformative Gaussian noise and generate actions through multi-step iteration, leading to high computational costs and failing to effectively leverage suboptimal trajectories in datasets. We propose MeanFlow2Better (MF2B), which reformulates offline RL as generative decision chunk optimization from current performance status to better performance status in a high-level latent decision space. MF2B maps trajectory chunks to high-level latent decision representations through an encoder, placing generation starting points within a structured decision space sculpted by behavioral data. The model learns a shared conditional mean velocity field in this space, constructing two flow mappings: from Gaussian-Induced Decision Sources toward positive behavioral decision regions, and from suboptimal decisions toward high-value decision regions. Both flow mappings complete decision refinement through one-step ODE integration over the unit time interval $Δt=1$, requiring neither multi-step iterative sampling nor backpropagation through time. Furthermore, we propose hierarchical chunk-level advantage to guide the flow direction toward high-return regions, where the high-level decisions cover multiple low-level actions, leading to sequence length compression from $T$ to $K \approx T/H$ and significantly lower computational overhead. On the D4RL Gym-MuJoCo benchmark, MF2B achieves an average score of 90.91, matching or surpassing strong baselines.
