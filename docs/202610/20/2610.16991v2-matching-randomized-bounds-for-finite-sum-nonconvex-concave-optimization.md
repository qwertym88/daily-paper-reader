## 摘要
我们刻画了具有有界对偶域的有限和型非凸-凹优化的随机分量预言机复杂度。对于 Φ(x)=max_{y∈Y} n^{-1}∑_{i=1}^n f_i(x,y)，目标是在至少 2/3 的概率下并在固定查询预算内，找到 Moreau 包络 Φ_{1/(2L)} 的一个 ε-稳定点。令 A=LΔ/ε^2，B=LD_Y/ε，且 B̄=max{1,B}，其中 Δ 界定初始原始间隙，D_Y 界定对偶直径。当 A 高于某个普适常数时，最坏情况复杂度为 Θ̃(n+A min{√n B̄+B̄^4, n^{3/4}B̄})。当 B≤n^{1/4} 时，这些界在常数因子内一致；当 B 更大时，则在相差一个对数因子的意义下一致。上界仅要求均方平均光滑性以及平均值关于对偶变量的凹性；下界在个体光滑性下即已成立。下界构造涵盖自适应分量选择、任意查询范数、私有随机化，以及从未被查询过的输出。锥构造给出小直径界，而串行分支构造与平衡复制给出其余两种情形。在上界方面，一种带有原始平均的持久估计器与配备显式原始证书的加速近端对偶步相辅相成。该刻画是在所有可容许的已知对偶域和有限维数上取最坏情况。

## Abstract
We characterize the randomized component-oracle complexity of finite-sum nonconvex-concave optimization with a bounded dual domain. For $Φ(x)=\max_{y\in Y}n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^n f_i(x,y)$, the goal is to find an $\varepsilon$-stationary point of the Moreau envelope $Φ_{1/(2L)}$ with probability at least $2/3$ and a fixed query budget. Let $A=LΔ/\varepsilon^2$, $B=LD_Y/\varepsilon$, and $\bar B=\max\{1,B\}$, where $Δ$ bounds the initial primal gap and $D_Y$ bounds the dual diameter. For $A$ above a universal constant, the worst-case complexity is \[ \widetildeΘ\!\left(n+A\min\left\{ \sqrt n\,\bar B+\bar B^4,\ n^{3/4}\bar B\right\}\right). \] The bounds agree within constants for $B\le n^{1/4}$ and within one logarithm for larger $B$. Upper bounds require only mean-squared averaged smoothness and concavity of the average in the dual variable; lower bounds already hold under individual smoothness. The lower constructions cover adaptive component selection, arbitrary query norms, private randomization, and outputs that were never queried. A cone construction gives the small-diameter bound, while a serial branch construction and balanced replication give the remaining two regimes. On the upper side, a persistent estimator with primal averaging complements accelerated proximal dual steps equipped with an explicit primal certificate. The characterization takes the worst case over admissible known dual domains and finite dimensions.
