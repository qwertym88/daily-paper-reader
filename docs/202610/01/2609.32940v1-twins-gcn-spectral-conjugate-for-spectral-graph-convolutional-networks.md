## 摘要
图卷积网络通过图移位算子进行重复局部聚合来传播信息；即，K 层网络可达到 K 跳邻域。一方面，这种扩散会导致过平滑。另一方面，长程依赖又需要这种深度。在长距离上无衰减地传输信息，要求移位算子能够区分流动方向；对称算子无法做到这一点，而有向算子可以满足。提取纯方向性的一种自然方式是通过笛卡尔分解取移位算子的斜对称部分，然而它通常不与移位本身对易，这意味着基于它构建的滤波器不是移位不变的。我们转而使用谱共轭；即，算子在映射 τ: z ↦ z 的共轭 下的像，它与移位对易，并将其分解为耗散部分和非耗散部分。由此得到两个滤波器族：求和滤波器，其非耗散分量无能量损耗地传输信号；以及比值滤波器，其形式是分量对的比值，而非移位的多项式。两者均来自非全纯核，因而位于经典谱卷积所依据的全纯类之外。在有向循环上，比值滤波器变为具有全局冲激响应的 IIR 滤波器；我们证明，相对于每个 K 次多项式滤波器，它具有长程可达性差距。切比雪夫重参数化给出了具有实系数的稳定顶点域层，从而得到 TwinS-GCN；该模型以更小的深度解决图迁移任务，并在节点分类基准上与最先进的图卷积网络相比具有竞争力。

## Abstract
Graph convolutional networks propagate information by repeated local aggregation through a graph shift operator; i.e., a $K$-layer network reaches $K$ hops neighborhood. On the one hand, such spreading can lead to oversmoothing. On the other hand, long-range dependencies demand the depth. Transporting information on long distances and without attenuation requires the shift to distinguish a direction of flow, which a symmetric operator cannot perform but a directed one can fulfill. A natural way to extract pure-directionality is to take the skew-symmetric part of the shift operator through the Cartesian split, which, however, generally does not commute with the shift itself, meaning that the filters built on it are not shift-invariant. We instead use the spectral conjugate; i.e., the image of the operator under $τ:z\mapsto \bar{z}$, which commutes with the shift and splits it into a dissipative and a non-dissipative part. Two filter families follow: a sum filter, whose non-dissipative component transports signal without energy loss, and a ratio filter, ratio in the pair of components rather than polynomial in the shift. Both arise from non-holomorphic kernels, placing them outside the holomorphic class underlying classical spectral convolution. On the directed cycle, the ratio filter becomes an IIR filter with global impulse response, for which we prove a long-range reach gap against every degree-$K$ polynomial filter. Chebyshev reparameterization gives stable vertex-domain layers with real coefficients, yielding TwinS-GCN, which solves graph transfer tasks at reduced depth and is competitive with state-of-the-art graph convolutional networks on node classification benchmarks.
