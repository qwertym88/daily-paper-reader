## 摘要
本文研究由具有速度相关约束几何的隐式扫掠过程所支配的最优控制问题，其中动力学由涉及移动凸多面体集的法锥的微分包含描述。由于状态、速度和约束反作用之间的隐式耦合，此类系统构成了一类具有挑战性的非光滑微分包含。为处理这些困难，我们采用离散逼近方法，并结合变分分析和广义微分工具。该方法使我们能够推导离散时间问题中局部极小点的必要最优性条件，随后通过取极限得到原连续时间系统的最优性条件。所得结果将已有的扫掠过程最优控制理论推广到一类具有状态和速度相关约束的隐式动力系统。

## Abstract
This paper studies optimal control problems governed by an implicit sweeping process with velocity-dependent constraint geometry, where the dynamics are described by a differential inclusion involving the normal cone to a moving convex polyhedral set. Such systems form a challenging class of nonsmooth differential inclusions due to the implicit coupling between the state, velocity, and constraint reaction. To address these difficulties, we use a method of discrete approximations together with tools of variational analysis and generalized differentiation. This approach allows us to derive necessary optimality conditions for local minimizer in discrete-time problems and subsequently pass to the limit to obtain optimality conditions for the original continuous-time system. The obtained results extend existing optimal control theory for sweeping processes to a class of implicit dynamical systems with state-and velocity-dependent constraints.
