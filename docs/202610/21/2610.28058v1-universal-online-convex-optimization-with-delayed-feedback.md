## 摘要
我们研究带延迟反馈的通用在线凸优化。我们提出一种单一算法，能够同时适应一般凸、强凸和指数凹损失，而无需事先知道曲率类别或参数。我们的遗憾界在时间跨度 T 和延迟依赖上达到已知最佳，最多相差双对数因子：凸损失约为 sqrt(T + d_tot)，曲率损失为 σ_max log T 速率，其中 d_tot 是总累积延迟，σ_max 是同时缺失观测的最大数量。我们的主要技术贡献是带二阶超额损失保证的延迟元学习器，它能够对延迟基学习器进行曲率自适应聚合。此外，我们新的指数凹基学习器产生更紧的遗憾界，通过用可能小得多的 σ_max 替代最大延迟，改进了最先进的延迟依赖，同时保留了互补的 sqrt(d_tot) 依赖。

## Abstract
We study universal online convex optimization with delayed feedback. We develop a single algorithm that simultaneously adapts to general convex, strongly convex, and exp-concave losses without prior knowledge of the curvature class or parameters. Our regret bounds match the best known dependence on the time horizon $T$ and delays up to doubly logarithmic factors: roughly $\sqrt{T + d_{\mathrm{tot}}}$ for convex losses and $σ_{\max}\log T$ rates with curved losses, where $d_{\mathrm{tot}}$ is the total cumulative delay and $σ_{\max}$ is the maximum number of simultaneously missing observations. Our main technical contribution is a delayed meta-learner with a second-order excess-loss guarantee, which enables curvature-adaptive aggregation of delayed base learners. In addition, our new exp-concave base learner yields a sharper regret bound which improves the state-of-the-art delay dependence by replacing the maximum delay with the potentially much smaller $σ_{\max}$, while retaining the complementary $\sqrt{d_{\mathrm{tot}}}$ dependence.
