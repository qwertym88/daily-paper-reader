## 摘要
我们研究用于无约束最小化光滑强凸目标函数的一阶优化方法的瞬态行为。这些方法被建模为有限时间区间上的线性系统，并嵌入到与梯度非线性的反馈连接中。通过离散时间 Zames-Falb 积分二次约束刻画函数类，我们采用鲁棒 H2 控制框架，获得凸半定规划，从而证明关于到优化器终端距离的紧最坏情况界。基于同一表述，我们推导出一种瞬态最优方法的凸综合过程，该方法达到最优有限时域界，并保证在任意指定步数后误差降低。该框架自然可扩展到梯度测量存在延迟的情形，从而能够进行瞬态性能分析，并综合反馈延迟下的最优算法。

## Abstract
We study the transient behavior of first-order optimization methods for unconstrained minimization of smooth and strongly convex objective functions. These methods are modelled as linear systems over finite-time intervals embedded in feedback with the gradient nonlinearity. By characterizing the function class through discrete-time Zames-Falb integral quadratic constraints, we employ a robust $\mathcal{H}_2$ control framework to obtain convex semidefinite programs certifying tight worst-case bounds on the terminal distance to the optimizer. Building on the same formulation, we derive a convex synthesis procedure for a transient-optimal method that achieves the optimal finite-horizon bounds and guarantees error reduction over any prescribed number of steps. The framework naturally extends to delayed gradient measurements, enabling both transient performance analysis and synthesis of optimal algorithms under feedback delays.
