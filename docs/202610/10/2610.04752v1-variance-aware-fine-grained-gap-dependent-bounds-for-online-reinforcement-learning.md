## 摘要
我们研究面向分幕式表格马尔可夫决策过程的无模型在线强化学习（RL），同时关注间隙依赖遗憾和策略切换成本。尽管针对使用 Hoeffding 型探索奖励的无模型 RL 算法，细粒度间隙依赖分析已经建立，但对于采用基于方差的探索奖励的无模型算法，此类结果仍然未知，尽管它们具有更优的最坏情况保证和粗粒度间隙依赖保证。本文通过为 UCB-Bernstein+（一种改进的 UCB-Bernstein 算法）建立方差感知无模型在线 RL 中的首个细粒度间隙依赖遗憾上界，解决了这一开放问题。此外，通过将分阶段策略更新设计融入我们的细粒度框架，并使用改进的基于方差的探索奖励，我们实现了迄今为止已知最优的间隙依赖局部切换成本。此外，我们的分析在遗憾和局部切换成本两方面均较原始 UCB-Bernstein 算法给出了改进的最坏情况保证。数值实验进一步表明，UCB-Bernstein+ 在遗憾和局部切换成本方面均取得了良好的经验性能。

## Abstract
We study model-free online reinforcement learning (RL) for episodic tabular Markov decision processes, focusing on both gap-dependent regret and policy switching cost. While fine-grained gap-dependent analysis has been established for model-free RL algorithms using Hoeffding-type exploration bonuses, such results for model-free algorithms with variance-based exploration bonuses remain unknown, despite their superior worst-case and coarse-grained gap-dependent guarantees. In this paper, we resolve this open problem by establishing the first fine-grained gap-dependent regret upper bound for UCB-Bernstein+, a refined UCB-Bernstein algorithm, in variance-aware model-free online RL. Moreover, by integrating a stage-wise policy update design into our fine-grained framework and using refined variance-based bonuses, we achieve the best-known gap-dependent local switching cost to date. In addition, our analysis yields improved worst-case guarantees for both regret and local switching cost over the original UCB-Bernstein algorithm. Numerical experiments further demonstrate that UCB-Bernstein+ achieves favorable empirical performance in both regret and local switching cost.
